# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal pp. 47–48 (weekday festal Lauds canticle)
!1 Chronicles 29:10-13

BLESSED be thou, Lord God of Israel our father, * for ever and ever.
2. Thine, O Lord, is the greatness, and the power, and the glory, † and the victory, and the majesty: * for all that is in the heaven and in the earth is thine;
3. Thine is the kingdom, O Lord, * and thou art exalted as head above all.
4. Both riches and honour come of thee, * and thou reignest over all;
5. And in thine hand is power and might, * and in thine hand it is to make great, and to give strength unto all.
6. Now therefore, our God, we thank thee, * and praise thy glorious Name.
