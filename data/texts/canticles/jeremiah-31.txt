# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal pp. 65 (weekday festal Lauds canticle)
!Jeremiah 31:10-14

HEAR the word of the Lord, O ye nations, * and declare it in the isles afar off,
2. And say, He that scattered Israel will gather him, * and keep him, as a shepherd doth his flock.
3. For the Lord hath redeemed Jacob, * and ransomed him from the hand of him that was stronger than he.
4. Therefore they shall come and sing in the height of Sion, * and shall flow together to the goodness of the Lord,
5. For wheat, and for wine, and for oil, * and for the young of the flock and of the herd:
6. And their soul shall be as a watered garden; * and they shall not sorrow any more at all.
7. Then shall the virgin rejoice in the dance, * both young men and old together:
8. For I will turn their mourning into joy, * and will comfort them, and make them rejoice from their sorrow.
9. And I will satiate the soul of the priests with fatness, * and my people shall be satisfied with my goodness, saith the Lord.
