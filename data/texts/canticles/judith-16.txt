# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal pp. 58–59 (weekday festal Lauds canticle)
!Judith 16:13-17

I WILL sing unto the Lord a new song: * O Lord, thou art great and glorious, wonderful in strength, and invincible.
2. Let all creatures serve thee: * for thou spakest, and they were made;
3. Thou didst send forth thy spirit, and it created them, * and there is none that can resist thy voice.
4. For the mountains shall be moved from their foundations with the waters, † the rocks shall melt as wax at thy presence: * yet thou art merciful to them that fear thee.
5. For all sacrifice is too little for a sweet savour unto thee: * but he that feareth the Lord is great at all times.
6. Woe to the nations that rise up against my kindred! * the Lord Almighty will take vengeance of them in the day of judgment.
