!Habakkuk 3:2-19

O Lord, I have heard thy speech, * and was afraid.
2. O Lord, revive thy work in the midst of the years, in the midst of the years make known; * in wrath remember mercy.
3. God came from Teman, * and the Holy One from mount Paran.
4. His glory covered the heavens, * and the earth was full of his praise.
5. And his brightness was as the light; he had horns coming out of his hand: * and there was the hiding of his power.
6. Before him went the pestilence, * and burning coals went forth at his feet.
7. He stood, and measured the earth: * he beheld, and drove asunder the nations;
8. And the everlasting mountains were scattered, the perpetual hills did bow: * his ways are everlasting.
9. I saw the tents of Cushan in affliction: * and the curtains of the land of Midian did tremble.
10. Was the Lord displeased against the rivers? * was thine anger against the rivers?
11. Was thy wrath against the sea, * that thou didst ride upon thine horses and thy chariots of salvation?
12. Thy bow was made quite naked, * according to the oaths of the tribes, even thy word.
13. Thou didst cleave the earth with rivers: * the mountains saw thee, and they trembled:
14. The overflowing of the water passed by: * the deep uttered his voice, and lifted up his hands on high.
15. The sun and moon stood still in their habitation: * at the light of thine arrows they went, and at the shining of thy glittering spear.
16. Thou didst march through the land in indignation, * thou didst thresh the heathen in anger.
17. Thou wentest forth for the salvation of thy people, * even for salvation with thine Anointed;
18. Thou woundedst the head out of the house of the wicked, * by discovering the foundation unto the neck.
19. Thou didst strike through with his staves the head of his villages; * they came out as a whirlwind to scatter me: their rejoicing was as to devour the poor secretly.
20. Thou didst walk through the sea with thine horses, * through the heap of great waters.
21. When I heard, my belly trembled; * my lips quivered at the voice:
22. Rottenness entered into my bones, * and I trembled in myself, that I might rest in the day of trouble:
23. When he cometh up unto the people, * he will invade them with his troops.
24. Although the fig tree shall not blossom, * neither shall fruit be in the vines;
25. The labour of the olive shall fail, * and the fields shall yield no meat;
26. The flock shall be cut off from the fold, * and there shall be no herd in the stalls:
27. Yet I will rejoice in the Lord, * I will joy in the God of my salvation.
28. The Lord God is my strength, and he will make my feet like hinds' feet, * and he will make me to walk upon mine high places.
