# Collect conclusions, per XII Of the Collects in the Monastic Diurnal's General
# Rubrics (PDF p. 29, printed p. xxxi; also GRMB XXXIII.4):
#
#   If the Collect is addressed to God the Father, the conclusion is Through
#   Jesus Christ; if addressed to the God the Son, the conclusion is Who livest
#   and reignest.
#   If, in the beginning of the Collect, the Son is mentioned, Through the same
#   Jesus Christ is said; but if the Son is mentioned at the end of the Collect,
#   Who liveth and reigneth is said.
#   If mention is made of the Holy Spirit in the Collect, there is said In the
#   unity of the same Holy Ghost, etc.
#
# Which form each collect takes is recorded in shared/collect-conclusions.
# Wording follows the diurnal. The books print these abbreviated ("Through
# &c."); we expand them, as we do the secret prayers, because the ink and space
# constraints that justify abbreviating in print do not apply here.

[collect-conclusion-through]
Through Jesus Christ thy Son, our Lord, who with thee in the unity of the Holy
Ghost, liveth and reigneth God, world without end.
R. Amen.

[collect-conclusion-through-spirit]
Through Jesus Christ thy Son, our Lord, who with thee in the unity of the same
Holy Ghost, liveth and reigneth God, world without end.
R. Amen.

[collect-conclusion-through-same]
Through the same thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord, who with thee in the unity of
the Holy Spirit, liveth and reigneth God, world without end.
R. Amen.

[collect-conclusion-through-same-spirit]
Through the same thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord, who with thee in the unity of
the same Holy Spirit, liveth and reigneth God, world without end.
R. Amen.

[collect-conclusion-who-liveth]
Who with thee liveth and reigneth in the unity of the Holy Ghost, God, world
without end.
R. Amen.

# Second person, for a collect addressed to the Son. The parish books print this
# one expanded in at least one place (All Souls): "Who with God the Father in the
# unity of the Holy Spirit livest and reignest God, world without end." We keep
# "Holy Ghost" so all six forms read alike in the same office; the books and the
# diurnal differ on that word throughout, and the rest of this set follows the
# diurnal.
[collect-conclusion-who-livest]
Who with God the Father in the unity of the Holy Ghost livest and reignest God,
world without end.
R. Amen.

[collect-conclusion-who-livest-spirit]
Who with God the Father in the unity of the same Holy Ghost livest and reignest
God, world without end.
R. Amen.

[collect-conclusion-who-liveth-spirit]
Who with thee liveth and reigneth in the unity of the same Holy Ghost, God,
world without end.
R. Amen.
