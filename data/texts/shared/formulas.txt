# Collect conclusions, per XII Of the Collects in the Monastic Diurnal's General
# Rubrics (PDF p. 29, printed p. xxxi; also GRMB XXXIII.4):
#
#   If the Collect is addressed to God the Father, the conclusion is Through
#   Jesus Christ; if addressed to the God the Son, the conclusion is Who livest
#   and reignest.
#   If, in the beginning of the Collect, the Son is mentioned, Through the same
#   Jesus Christ is said; but if the Son is mentioned at the end of the Collect,
#   Who liveth and reigneth is said.
#   If mention is made of the Holy Spirit in the Collect, there is said In the
#   unity of the same Holy Ghost, etc.
#
# Which form each collect takes is recorded in shared/collect-conclusions.
# Wording follows the diurnal, which prints all five forms in full at XII and
# says "Holy Spirit" in every one of them. The books print the conclusions
# abbreviated ("Through &c."); we expand them, as we do the secret prayers,
# because the ink and space constraints that justify abbreviating in print do
# not apply here.
#
# The body of the diurnal prints a conclusion in full in only two places, and
# they disagree: the Office of the Dead (printed pp. 33*, 42*, and the All
# Souls collect) says "Holy Spirit", agreeing with the rule, while Prime
# (printed p. 9) says "Holy Ghost". The rule and the larger group govern here;
# ordinary/prime/collect keeps its printed wording inline and is flagged there.
