[triduum-hours-opening-rubric]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 314 (Maundy Thursday at the Hours)
The Our Father and Hail Mary (and at Prime I believe) having been said secretly,
the Hour is begun at once with the Psalms, without the opening Versicles or the
Hymn.

[triduum-christus-factus-rubric]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 313 (Maundy Thursday at Lauds)
When the Antiphon Christ, for our sake is begun, all kneel; and when it is ended
the Our Father is said entirely in secret. Then in a somewhat higher voice is
said Psalm 51.

[triduum-collect-rubric]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 313 (Maundy Thursday at Lauds)
The Psalm being ended, there is said, without Let us pray, in a low voice, the
Collect of the day, its conclusion Who with thee ... liveth being said in
silence. Thus are ended all the Hours during this Triduum, through None of Holy
Saturday.

[triduum-prime-ending-rubric]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 314 (Maundy Thursday at Prime)
During this Triduum the Martyrology is not read, and the rest until the end of
the Hour is not said.

[triduum-lauds-noise-rubric]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 313
But at Lauds, after the Collect is ended, a little noise and clatter is made; the lighted candle is at once brought forth from beneath the Altar; and all rise and depart in silence.

[triduum-compline-opening-rubric]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 316 (Good Friday at Matins, At Compline)
Sir, ask a blessing is not said, nor the Short Lesson, nor Our help, nor the Lord's Prayer; but the Officiant begins the Confession immediately. After the Confession and Absolution the Psalms are begun at once.

[holy-saturday-compline-omission-rubric]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 361 (Easter Even at Compline)
The Hymn, Chapter, and Versicle are not said, but when the Psalms are ended the
Antiphon on the Canticle Nunc dimittis is begun.

[holy-saturday-compline-alleluia-rubric]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 361 (Easter Even at Compline)
Henceforth Praise be to thee, O Lord, King of eternal glory is not said, but in
its place is said Alleluia.

[holy-saturday-compline-collect-rubric]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 362 (Easter Even at Compline)
Then is said at once:

[triduum-lauds-omission-rubric]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 311 (Maundy Thursday at Lauds)
The Chapter, Short Responsory, and Hymn are not said during this Triduum.

# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 315 (Maundy Thursday at Vespers). The
