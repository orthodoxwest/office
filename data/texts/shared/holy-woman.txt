[antiphon-the-kingdom-of-heaven]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 674 (Common of Holy Women not Virgins)
The kingdom of heaven * is likened unto a merchantman seeking goodly pearls; who, when he had found one pearl of great price, went and sold all that he had, and bought it.

[versicle-in-thy-grace]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 673 (Common of Holy Women not Virgins)
V. In thy grace and in thy beauty.
R. Go forth, ride prosperously, and reign.
