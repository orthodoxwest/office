# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 21* (Common of One Martyr out of Paschaltide, At Lauds)
Whosoever shall confess me * before men, him will I confess also before my Father.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 21* (Common of One Martyr out of Paschaltide, At Lauds)
He that followeth me * shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life, saith the Lord.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 21* (Common of One Martyr out of Paschaltide, At Lauds)
If any man serve me, * let him follow me; and where I am, there shall also my servant be.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 21* (Common of One Martyr out of Paschaltide, At Lauds)
If any man have served me, * he shall be honoured of my Father which is in heaven, saith the Lord.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 21* (Common of One Martyr out of Paschaltide, At Lauds)
Father, * I will that where I am, there may also my servant be.
