# Paschal variant of the Common of Virgins (Virgin Martyrs).
# Sections not present here fall through to the regular commons file.
#
# Per the Monastic Diurnal's General Rubric on the Common of Saints (p. 6*, §3),
# in the Common of Virgins all is said as out of Paschaltide except that one
# Alleluia is added to each Antiphon, Versicle and Response, and the Short
# Responsories of Lauds and Vespers take a double Alleluia. The draft
# archdiocesan Commons Lauds book prints the same rule inline as
# "(P.T. Alleluia)" (pp. 74-84).

[psalm-antiphon]
@use commons/virgin-martyr-paschal/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Antiphon)
This is a wise Virgin, * and one of the number of the prudent, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Antiphon)
This is a wise Virgin, * whom the Lord hath found watching, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Antiphon)
Blessed is the barren * that is undefiled; she shall have fruit in the visitation of holy souls, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# Already ends with Alleluia out of Paschaltide (monastic diurnal, p. 47*).
@use commons/virgin-martyr/psalm-antiphon-4

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Antiphon)
This woman * is beautiful among the daughters of Jerusalem, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-4-vespers]
@use commons/virgin-martyr-paschal/psalm-antiphon-5

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Antiphon)
The kingdom of heaven * is likened unto a merchantman seeking goodly pearls; who, when he had found one pearl of great price, went and sold all that he had, and bought it, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Antiphon)
Come, thou Bride of Christ, * receive the crown which the Lord hath prepared for thee for ever, alleluia.

[short-responsory-lauds]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 48* (Common of Virgins, "In Paschaltide Short R.")
R. In thy grace and in thy beauty, * alleluia, alleluia.
R. In thy grace and in thy beauty, * alleluia, alleluia.
V. Go forth, ride prosperously, and reign.
R. Alleluia, alleluia.
Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.
R. In thy grace and in thy beauty, * alleluia, alleluia.

[short-responsory-vespers]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 47* (Common of Virgins, "In Paschaltide Short R.")
R. God shall help her with his countenance, * alleluia, alleluia.
R. God shall help her with his countenance, * alleluia, alleluia.
V. God is in the midst of her, therefore shall she not be removed.
R. Alleluia, alleluia.
Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Ghost.
R. God shall help her with his countenance, * alleluia, alleluia.

[versicle-lauds]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Versicle and Response)
V. Full of grace are thy lips, alleluia.
R. Because God hath blessed thee for ever, alleluia.

[versicle-first-vespers]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Versicle and Response)
V. In thy grace and in thy beauty, alleluia.
R. Go forth, ride prosperously, and reign, alleluia.
