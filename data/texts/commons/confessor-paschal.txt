# Paschal variant of the Common of a Confessor not a Bishop.
# Sections not present here fall through to the regular commons file.
#
# Per the Monastic Diurnal's General Rubric on the Common of Saints (p. 6*, §3),
# in the Commons of Confessors, Virgins and Holy Women all is said as out of
# Paschaltide except that one Alleluia is added to each Antiphon, Versicle and
# Response, and the Short Responsories of Lauds and Vespers take a double
# Alleluia. The draft archdiocesan Commons Lauds book prints the same rule
# inline as "(P.T. Alleluia)" after each antiphon (pp. 61-66).

[psalm-antiphon]
@use commons/confessor-paschal/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Commons Lauds book, p. 61 (Common of a Confessor not a Bishop, "(P.T. alleluia)")
Lord, thou deliveredst * unto me five talents: behold, I have gained beside them five talents more, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Commons Lauds book, p. 62 (Common of a Confessor not a Bishop, "(p.t. alleluia)")
Well done, thou good servant, * in little things found faithful, enter thou into the joy of thy Lord, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Commons Lauds book, p. 63 (Common of a Confessor not a Bishop, "(P.T. alleluia)")
A wise and faithful servant, * whom the Lord hath made ruler over all his household, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Commons Lauds book, p. 63 (Common of a Confessor not a Bishop, "P.T. Alleluia")
Blessed is that servant * whom his Lord, when he cometh and knocketh at the door, shall find watching, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Commons Lauds book, p. 66 (Common of a Confessor not a Bishop, "(p.t. Alleluia)")
Good and faithful servant, * enter thou into the joy of thy Lord, alleluia.

# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Commons Vespers book, p. 678 (Common of a Confessor not a Bishop, Paschaltide)

# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Commons Vespers book, p. 679 (Common of a Confessor not a Bishop, Paschaltide)

[psalm-antiphon-3-vespers]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Commons Vespers book, p. 680 (Common of a Confessor not a Bishop, Paschaltide)
A wise and faithful servant, * whom the Lord hath made ruler over all his household, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-4-vespers]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Commons Vespers book, p. 681 (Common of a Confessor not a Bishop, Paschaltide)

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Antiphon)
Well done, good and faithful servant, * thou hast been faithful over a few things, I will make thee ruler over many things: enter thou into the joy of thy Lord, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Antiphon)
I will liken him * unto a wise man, which built his house upon a rock, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 6* (General Rubric §3: one Alleluia added to each Antiphon)
The world, and all things earthly, * this man despised, triumphant: he laid up treasures in heaven by word and deed, alleluia.
