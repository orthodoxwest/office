# Paschal variants of the Common of Feasts of the B.V.M.: "one Alleluia is
# added to each Antiphon, Versicle, and Response" (Monastic Diurnal p. 6*, §3),
# in the printed form ", alleluia." (Common of Apostles in Paschaltide,
# pp. 16*-17*; #645). Only the antiphons of the Hours (pp. 65*, 67*) are here:
# the Office of Saint Mary on Saturday takes them in Paschaltide (pp. 69*, 71*)
# (#652). Sections not present here fall through to the regular commons file.

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, pp. 65*, 67* (Common of Feasts B.V.M., Ant. 1 at Lauds and Prime), with p. 6* §3
While the King * sitteth at his table, my spikenard sendeth forth a sweet-smelling savour, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, pp. 65*, 67* (Common of Feasts B.V.M., Ant. 2 at Lauds and Terce), with p. 6* §3
His left hand * is under my head, and his right hand doth embrace me, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, pp. 65*, 67* (Common of Feasts B.V.M., Ant. 3 at Lauds and Sext), with p. 6* §3
I am black, but comely, * O ye daughters of Jerusalem; therefore the King delighteth in me, and hath brought me into his chambers, alleluia.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, pp. 65*, 67* (Common of Feasts B.V.M., Ant. 5 at Lauds and None), with p. 6* §3
Thou art beautiful * and pleasant, O holy Mother of God, in thy felicity, alleluia.
