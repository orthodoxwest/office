[psalm-antiphon]
@use proper/st-clement-i/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 667 (St Clement, At Lauds)
While holy Clement * prayed, the Lamb of God appeared unto him.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 667 (St Clement, At Lauds)
Not for my own merits * did the Lord send me unto you, that I might be made partaker of your crowns.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 667 (St Clement, At Lauds)
Upon the mountain * I saw the Lamb standing, from beneath whose foot floweth a fount of living waters.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 667 (St Clement, At Lauds)
From underneath his foot * floweth a fount of living waters: a river, the streams whereof make glad the city of God.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 667 (St Clement, At Lauds)
All the nations * round about believed in Christ the Lord.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 667 (St Clement, At Lauds)
When he had started going to the sea, * the people cried out with loud voices, O Lord Jesus Christ, save him: and Clement responded, weeping, Heavenly Father, receive my spirit.

[collect]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 667 (St Clement, At Lauds)
O God, who makest us glad by the yearly festival of blessed Clement, thy Martyr and Bishop: mercifully grant that we who honour his birthday may imitate his fortitude in suffering.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 666–667 (St Clement, At I Vespers)
Let us all pray * to our Lord Jesus Christ, that he would open a fountain for his Confessors.
