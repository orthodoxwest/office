# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 776 (September 16, Ss Euphemia, Lucy and Geminian, Collect)
Grant, O Lord, that our prayers in this time of our rejoicing may be brought to good effect: that as with yearly service we recall the day of the passion of thy holy Martyrs, Euphemia, Lucy, and Geminian, so we may imitate the steadfastness of their faith.
