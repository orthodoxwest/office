[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 228 (Within the Octave of the Epiphany, III Day)
Three are the gifts * which the wise men presented unto the Lord: gold, frankincense, and myrrh, to the Son of God, to the mighty King, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 228 (Within the Octave of the Epiphany, III Day)
Light of light, * thou, O Christ, hast appeared, unto whom the wise men present their gifts, alleluia, alleluia.
