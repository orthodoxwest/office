# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 762 (August 28, St Hermes, Collect)
O God, who didst endue blessed Hermes thy Martyr with strength and constancy in his passion: grant unto us by his example; to despise for love of thee the prosperity of the world, and to fear none of its adversities.
