# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 662 (April 22, Ss Soter and Caius, Collect)
Protect us, O Lord, we beseech thee, who observe the feast of thy blessed Martyrs and Bishops Soter and Caius: and grant that by their meritorious supplication we may ever find favour in thy sight.
