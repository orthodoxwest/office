# The antiphons and versicles are the Common of Many Martyrs (Diurnal p. 484,
# "For theirs" and "The very hairs", p. 2*); the collect is the Forty's own.

[commemoration-collect]
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that we who acknowledge the fortitude of thy glorious Martyrs in their confession, may experience their loving intercession for us before thee.
