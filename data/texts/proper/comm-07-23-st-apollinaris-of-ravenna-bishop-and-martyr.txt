[commemoration-collect]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal p. 570, PDF p. 614, which prints no folio (July 23, St Apollinaris, B.M., Collect); ordo "Col. 570"
O God, the rewarder of faithful souls, who hast consecrated this day by the martyrdom of blessed Apollinaris, thy Priest: grant to us, we beseech thee, that we who keep his honoured festival may obtain pardon through his prayers.
