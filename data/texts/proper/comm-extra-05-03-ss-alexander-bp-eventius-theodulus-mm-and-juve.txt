# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 670 (May 3, commemoration of Ss Alexander, Eventius, Theodulus and Juvenal)
Grant, we beseech thee, almighty God: that we, who devoutly celebrate the birthday of thy Saints Alexander, Eventius, Theodulus and Juvenal; may, by their intercession, be delivered from all evils that beset us.
