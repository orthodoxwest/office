# SOURCE: monastic-diurnal-ordo-comms p. 655 (DI-6912d65ec4cd-Cwinifred-P699-45064eb5d548; commemoration-collect) — agent-proposed, not attested
Almighty everlasting God, who hast bestowed upon blessed Winifred the reward of her virginity: grant, we beseech thee, at her intercession, that we may put away the allurements of the world, and obtain with her a throne of everlasting glory.
