# Proper commemoration routing for St Helen, Empress.
# SOURCE: 2026 archdiocesan ordo, Aug. 17-18

[collect]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 591 (St Helen)
O Lord Jesus Christ, who didst reveal to blessed Helena the place where thy Cross lay hidden, that through her thy Church might be enriched with the precious treasure: grant us, at her intercession, that by the ransom of that life-giving wood, we may obtain the reward of everlasting life.
