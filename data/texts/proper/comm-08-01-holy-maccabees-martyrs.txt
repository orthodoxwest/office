# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 577 (St Peter's Chains, I Vespers, Commemoration of the Holy Maccabees; Lauds p. 579 "as above")
May the fraternal crown of thy victorious Martyrs bring us joy, O Lord: that our faith may receive an increase of strength, and that we may also have the comfort of their manifold intercessions.
