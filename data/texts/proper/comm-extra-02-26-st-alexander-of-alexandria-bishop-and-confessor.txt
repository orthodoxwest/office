[collect]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 36* (Another Collect, Common of a Confessor and Bishop; ordo Col. "O Lord")
O Lord, we beseech thee, hear the prayers which we offer thee on the solemnity of blessed Alexander, thy Bishop and Confessor: and by the interceding merits of him who worthily attained to serve thee, absolve us from all our sins.
