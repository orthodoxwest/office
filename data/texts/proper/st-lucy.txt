[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 451 (St Lucy, At I Vespers)
In thy patience * thou didst possess thy soul, O Lucy, spouse of Christ: thou didst hate the things which are in the world, and thou shinest among the Angels: resisting unto blood, thou didst vanquish the enemy.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 452 (St Lucy, At Lauds)
A pillar art thou * that may not be moved, O Lucy, spouse of Christ: and all the people are waiting until thou receive the crown of life, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 452 (St Lucy, At II Vespers)
With such gravity * was she endued by the Holy Spirit, that the virgin of the Lord remained unmoved.

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 451 (St Lucy, At Lauds)
While holy Lucy * prayed, there appeared unto her the blessed Agatha, who consoled the handmaid of Christ.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 451 (St Lucy, At Lauds)
O virgin Lucy, * why dost thou ask of me that which thou thyself canst presently bestow upon thy mother?

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 451 (St Lucy, At Lauds)
Through thee, O virgin Lucy, * the Syracusan city shall be glorified by the Lord Jesus Christ.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 451 (St Lucy, At Lauds)
I bless thee, * O Father of my Lord Jesus Christ: for by thy Son the fire is quenched round about thine handmaid.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 451 (St Lucy, At Lauds)
O my sister Lucy, * God's consecrated virgin, why dost thou ask of me that which thou thyself canst presently bestow upon thy mother?

[collect]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 452 (St Lucy, At Lauds)
Hear us, O God our Saviour: that as we rejoice in the festival of blessed Lucy, thy Virgin and Martyr, so we may be taught by the influence of her loving devotion.
