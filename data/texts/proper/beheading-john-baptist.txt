[psalm-antiphon]
@use proper/beheading-john-baptist/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 594 (Beheading of St John Baptist, At Lauds)
Herod himself had sent forth * and laid hold upon John, and bound him in prison, for Herodias' sake.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 594 (Beheading of St John Baptist, At Lauds)
My lord the king, * give me in a charger the head of John the Baptist.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 594 (Beheading of St John Baptist, At Lauds)
When the damsel danced, * her mother bade her: Ask for nothing else but the head of John.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 594 (Beheading of St John Baptist, At Lauds)
John reproved * Herod for the sake of Herodias, whom he took for wife from his brother Philip.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 594 (Beheading of St John Baptist, At Lauds)
Give me in a charger * the head of John the Baptist: and the king was exceeding sorry for his oath's sake.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 594 (Beheading of St John Baptist, At Lauds)
@use proper/beheading-john-baptist/magnificat-antiphon-first

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 595 (Beheading of St John Baptist, At II Vespers)
The king, the unbeliever, * sent his detestable servants, and commanded them to behead John the Baptist.

[collect]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 594 (Beheading of St John Baptist, At Lauds)
We beseech thee, O Lord, that the reverend festival of holy John Baptist thy Forerunner and Martyr: may bring upon us the benefit of thy saving help.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 594 (Beheading of St John Baptist, At I Vespers)
Herod sent * an executioner, and commanded him to behead John in the prison: and when his disciples heard of it, they came, and took up his body, and laid it in a tomb.
