[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 401 (Ember Friday within the Octave of Pentecost)
Jesus said: * But that ye may know that the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins, (he saith unto the sick of the palsy) I say unto thee, Arise, take up thy bed, and go thy way into thine house, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 401 (Thursday within the Octave; Ember Friday Vespers Mag is not printed separately)
But the Comforter, * which is the Holy Ghost, whom the Father will send in my Name, he will teach you all things, and bring all things to your remembrance, whatsoever I have said unto you, alleluia.
