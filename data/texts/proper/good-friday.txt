[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 336
God spared not * his own Son: but delivered him up for us all.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 336
My spirit * is vexed within me: and my heart within me is desolate.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 337
Said the one thief to the other, * We indeed receive the due reward of our deeds; but this man, what hath he done? Remember me, O Lord, when thou comest into thy kingdom.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 338
When my spirit is disquieted * within me, thou, O Lord, wilt remember mercy.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 340
Remember me, * O Lord my God, when thou comest into thy kingdom.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 341
They set up * over his head his accusation written: Jesus of Nazareth, the King of the Jews.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 341
When he had received * the vinegar, he said, It is finished: and he bowed his head, and gave up the ghost.

[commemoration-antiphon]
@use proper/good-friday/psalm-antiphon-1

[commemoration-versicle]
V. He hath set me in dark places.
R. As they that be dead of old.

# third days add their clauses to the first day's text.
