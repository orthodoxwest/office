[psalm-antiphon-1]
God spared not His Own Son * but delivered Him up for us all.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
My spirit is overwhelmed within me; * my heart within me is troubled.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
One thief said unto the other: * We indeed receive the due reward of our deeds, but what hath this man done? Lord, remember me, when thou comest into thy kingdom.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
Lord, when my soul is troubled, * thou wilt remember mercy.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
Lord, remember me * when thou comest into thy kingdom.

[benedictus-antiphon]
They set up over his head his accusation written: * Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews.

[magnificat-antiphon]
When He had received the vinegar, * he said: It is finished! and he bowed his Head, and gave up the Ghost.

@use proper/good-friday/psalm-antiphon-1

[commemoration-versicle]

[triduum-antiphon]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, printed p. 313 (Maundy Thursday at Lauds)
# Said after the psalmody at every Hour of the Triduum; the second and
# third days add their clauses to the first day's text.
Christ, ✠ for our sake, became obedient unto death, even the death of the cross.

[collect]
Look down, we beseech thee, O Lord, on this thy family, for which our Lord Jesus Christ did not hesitate to be delivered up into the hands of wicked men, and to suffer the torment of the Cross.

# seasonal/passiontide/chapter-vespers for the rubric and its witnesses. The
# slots stay hour-qualified because those two rubrics (Monastic Diurnal pp. 311
# and 315) speak only for Lauds and Vespers. The Little Hours have their own
# rubric on p. 314, modelled in the hour definitions rather than here: they omit
# the opening versicles and hymn outright and take a fixed psalmody, none of
# which a proper slot can express.
