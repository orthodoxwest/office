@use proper/good-friday/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
God spared not His Own Son * but delivered Him up for us all.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
My spirit is overwhelmed within me; * my heart within me is troubled.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
One thief said unto the other: * We indeed receive the due reward of our deeds, but what hath this man done? Lord, remember me, when thou comest into thy kingdom.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
Lord, when my soul is troubled, * thou wilt remember mercy.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
Lord, remember me * when thou comest into thy kingdom.

[benedictus-antiphon]
They set up over his head his accusation written: * Jesus of Nazareth, King of the Jews.

[magnificat-antiphon]
When He had received the vinegar, * he said: It is finished! and he bowed his Head, and gave up the Ghost.

[commemoration-versicle]
