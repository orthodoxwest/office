# St Anne is kept alone on 26 July; St Joachim stands alone on 16 August.
# The diurnal prints two forms of 26 July back to back: the joint feast "Sts
# Joachim and Anne, Parents of the B.V.M." (D2, full propers, the hymn Gaude
# mater Anna, and a plural commemoration antiphon "The Lord gave them the
# blessing of all nations"), and, following it, "St Anne, Mother of the B.V.M."
# — "all as in the Common, p. 51*, except" a proper collect. The ordo uses the
# latter: "Lauds W / As in Com. of Holy Women (51*), except / Col. (575)".
# So the joint feast's propers are deliberately NOT entered here, and this
# feast has no proper Benedictus antiphon — Lauds is the common throughout.
#
# The commemoration at II Vespers of St James (25 July) is resolved by the
# engine's following-office rule (XIV.14) from commons/holy-woman, giving
# "The kingdom of heaven" — matching the ordo's "Comm. Anne ('The kingdom of
# heaven' 5*; Col. 575)". Ordos 2017-2022 instead used a supplement antiphon,
# "Rejoice, O glorious matron"; 2023-2026 consistently use the common.

O God, who didst bestow such grace upon blessed Anne that she became the worthy mother of her who brought forth thine only-begotten Son: mercifully grant that we who celebrate her festival may be aided by her prayers before thee.
