# Lauds of the Dead, antiphons 1-5 (Monastic Diurnal pp. 99*-104*).
# The psalms they carry are laid out in data/office/lauds.txt [Psalmody-Dead].

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 99* (Lauds of the Dead)
The bones which thou hast broken * shall rejoice in the Lord.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 101* (Lauds of the Dead)
Thou, O Lord, * that hearest the prayer, unto thee shall all flesh come.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 102* (Lauds of the Dead)
Thy right hand * hath upholden me, O Lord.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 103* (Lauds of the Dead)
From the gate of hell, * deliver my soul, O Lord.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 104* (Lauds of the Dead)
Let everything that hath breath * praise the Lord.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 104* (Lauds of the Dead, Ant. on Benedictus)
I am the resurrection * and the life, saith the Lord: he that believeth in me, though he were dead, yet shall he live: and whosoever liveth and believeth in me shall never die.

# Vespers of the Dead, antiphons 1-5 (Monastic Diurnal pp. 72*-75*).

[psalm-antiphon-1-vespers]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 72* (Vespers of the Dead)
I will walk before the Lord * in the land of the living.

[psalm-antiphon-2-vespers]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 73* (Vespers of the Dead)
Woe is me * that I am constrained to dwell with Mesech.

[psalm-antiphon-3-vespers]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 74* (Vespers of the Dead)
The Lord shall preserve thee from all evil: * yea, it is even he that shall keep thy soul.

[psalm-antiphon-4-vespers]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 74* (Vespers of the Dead)
If thou, Lord, wilt be extreme to mark what is done amiss, * O Lord, who may abide it?

[psalm-antiphon-5-vespers]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 75* (Vespers of the Dead)
Despise not then, * O Lord, the works of thine own hands.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 75* (Vespers of the Dead, Ant. on Magnificat)
All that the Father giveth me * shall come to me; and him that cometh to me, I will in no wise cast out.

# The Office of the Dead has no chapter, hymn or short responsory at either
# hour: Lauds "begins at once" with the first antiphon (Monastic Diurnal
# p. 99*) and runs psalmody -> versicle -> Benedictus antiphon -> Our Father
# -> collect; Vespers likewise (pp. 72*-77*).

# The same versicle at both hours, but do NOT collapse these into one @use:
# the book really does punctuate them differently, "from heaven saying" at
# Lauds and "from heaven, saying" at Vespers. Both were read from the page
# images and re-checked.

[versicle-lauds]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 104* (Lauds of the Dead)
V. I heard a voice from heaven saying unto me.
R. Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord.

[versicle-vespers]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 75* (Vespers of the Dead)
V. I heard a voice from heaven, saying unto me.
R. Blessed are the dead which die in the Lord.

[collect]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 77* (The Office of the Dead, Collects)
# The ordo appoints the first collect only on Nov 2 ("1st Col. only, MD 77*").
O God, the Creator and Redeemer of all the faithful: grant unto the souls of thy servants and handmaids the remission of all their sins; that through devout supplications they may obtain the pardon they have always desired.

[compline-collect]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 643 (All Souls, At Compline)
# Printed conclusion "Through."
Look down, we beseech thee, O Lord, in merciful forgiveness upon the souls of all thy servants and handmaids, for whom we humbly entreat thy Majesty: that through this office of devout supplication they may be counted worthy to enter into everlasting rest.
Through thy Son Jesus Christ our Lord, who with thee in the unity of the Holy Spirit, liveth and reigneth God, world without end.
R. Amen.
