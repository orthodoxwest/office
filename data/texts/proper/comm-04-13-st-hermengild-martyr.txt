# SOURCE: supplemental-collects-2026-11.pdf ("Collects for Supplemental Offices"), PDF p. 2 (April 13, St Hermenegild)
O God, who didst teach thy blessed Martyr Hermenegild to prefer thy heavenly to an earthly kingdom: grant, we pray thee; that, following his pattern, we may despise all things that are transitory, and follow stedfastly after things eternal.
