[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 81 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
Right worthy of honour * is blessed John the Apostle, who leaned on the Lord's bosom at the last supper.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 82 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
This is that disciple * which testifieth of these things: and we know that his testimony is true.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 82 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
This is my disciple * if I will that he tarry till I come.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 83 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
There be some standing here * which shall not taste of death till they see the Son of man in his kingdom.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 85 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
Behold, my servant, mine elect, * whom I have chosen: I have put my spirit upon him.

[psalm-antiphon-1-vespers]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 70 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
In the day of thy power * shall the people offer thee free-will offerings with an holy worship: the dew of thy birth is of the womb of the morning.

[psalm-antiphon-2-vespers]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 71 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
The Lord hath sent * redemption unto his people: he hath commanded his covenant for ever.

[psalm-antiphon-3-vespers]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 72 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
Unto the godly * there ariseth up light in the darkness: the Lord is merciful, loving, and righteous.

[psalm-antiphon-4-vespers]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 73 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
With the Lord * there is mercy, and with him is plenteous redemption.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 89 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
This is the same John, * who leaned on the Lord's bosom at the last supper: the blessed Apostle, unto whom were revealed the secret things of heaven.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 76 (Saint John, Apostle and Evangelist)
There went this saying abroad * among the brethren, that that disciple should not die: yet Jesus said not unto him, He shall not die; but, if I will that he tarry till I come.

@use proper/st-john-evangelist/benedictus-antiphon

[commemoration-collect]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 68 (Commemoration of Saint John)
Merciful Lord, we beseech thee to cast thy bright beams of light upon thy Church; that it, being illumined by the doctrine of thy blessed Apostle and Evangelist Saint John, may so walk in the light of thy truth, that it may at length attain to life everlasting.

[collect]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds/Vespers books, pp. 90, 77
O Lord, graciously illumine thy Church; that, being enlightened by the doctrine of blessed John, thy Apostle and Evangelist, she may attain unto everlasting gifts.

He that feareth the Lord will do good; and he that hath the knowledge of the law shall obtain Wisdom, and as a mother shall she meet him.

With the bread of understanding shall she feed him, * and give him the water of wisdom to drink.
R. Thanks be to God.

# SOURCE: diurnal p. 198
Exsultet cælum laudibus

LET heaven's exultant praises ring,
And earth with joy responsive sing:
The Apostle's deeds and high estate
This festal-tide we celebrate.

O ye who, throned in glory dread,
Shall judge the living and the dead,
True lights, the world illumining,
Regard the suppliant prayer we bring.
