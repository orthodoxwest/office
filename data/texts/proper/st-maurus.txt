[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 456 (January 15, At I Vespers)
O most blessed of men! * who, rejecting this world, bore the yoke of Holy Rule from tender years so lovingly; and being made obedient even unto death, he denied himself, that he might wholly cling to Christ his Master, alleluia.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 457 — "Ant. on Benedictus. O most blessed of men, as above at I Vespers."
@use proper/st-maurus/magnificat-antiphon-first

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 458 (January 15, At II Vespers)
To-day holy Maurus, * lying upon a goat-skin, died happily before the altar; to-day the first-begotten disciple of blessed Benedict, through the guiding of the Holy Rule, came up to Christ, rising untroubled, accompanied by choirs of Angels; to-day the obedient man, telling his victories, was worthy to be crowned by the Lord, alleluia.
