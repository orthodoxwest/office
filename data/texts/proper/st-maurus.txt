[collect]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 457
O God, who for a pattern of obedience didst cause blessed Maurus to walk dry-shod upon the waters: grant that we may both follow perfectly the example of his virtues, and also be worthy to share in his reward.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 457 (Lauds: antiphon as at I Vespers, p. 456)
@use proper/st-maurus/magnificat-antiphon-first

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 458
To-day holy Maurus, * lying upon a goat-skin, died happily before the altar; to-day the first-begotten disciple of blessed Benedict, through the guiding of the Holy Rule, came up to Christ, rising untroubled, accompanied by choirs of Angels; to-day the obedient man, telling his victories, was worthy to be crowned by the Lord, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 456
O most blessed of men! * who, rejecting this world, bore the yoke of Holy Rule from tender years so lovingly; and being made obedient even unto death, he denied himself, that he might wholly cling to Christ his Master, alleluia.

# [psalm-antiphon]
# Single antiphon for all five psalms (fallback when psalm-antiphon-N is absent).
#
[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: diurnal pp. 456-457
The blessed Maurus, * born of a renowned and noble house, from his boyhood esteemed the reproach of Christ greater riches than the treasures of the world.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 457
Upheld by wings of obedience, * he walked upon the waters; and borne by the Spirit of God, he was saved from sinking in the flood.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 457
The blessed Maurus, * a disciple of holy Benedict from tender years, stood forth as a zealous follower of his master's excellence.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 457
From the house of prayer * he passed over unto the place of the wondrous tabernacle, even unto the house of God, with exceeding love of whom he burned.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 457
He was chosen * by the Lord to be an example to the cloistered, and a chief observer of the Holy Rule.

[hymn-vespers]
# SOURCE: diurnal pp. 455-456 (At I Vespers; II Vespers "All as at I Vespers", p. 458)
Qui te, posthabitis

Defender, leader true, thine own companions deemed
Thy splendour half divine, since all were less esteemed:
For thy most worthy deeds, Maurus, accept the lays
Wherewith we celebrate thy praise.

Born of a noble stock, great honour was his due,
But palaces he spurned, and from the world withdrew;
Delights he trampled down, estates and robes unpriced,
To undergo the yoke of Christ.

The holy Abbot's grace, before his eyes displayed,
By deeds of equal worth he eagerly portrayed;
The pattern of the life monastic shone in truth
From every action of the youth.

Sternly, with sackcloth rough, self-mastery he wrought,
And by the curb of law, unbroken silence sought;
The ever-watchful nights in fervent prayer he spent;
Whole days of fasting underwent.

Right speedily he flew to do the father's hest,
Dry-shod, the waters deep with fearless feet he pressed;
And safely he returned with Placidus, set free
Like Peter walking on the sea.

To thee, O Trinity, high praise and honour be,
Whose countenance desired the heaven-dwellers see:
Grant that the Holy Rule may be our pathway plain
The prize of Maurus to attain.

Amen.
