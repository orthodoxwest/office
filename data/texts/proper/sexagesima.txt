[psalm-antiphon]
@use proper/sexagesima/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 155 (Sexagesima Sunday)
After thy great goodness, * have mercy upon me, O God.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 156 (Sexagesima Sunday)
If the Lord be on my side, * I will not fear what man doeth unto me.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 158 (Sexagesima Sunday)
Within the veil * have I called very early in the morning: O God, thou art my God, early will I seek thee.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 159 (Sexagesima Sunday)
Let us sing a hymn * unto the Lord our God.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 160 (Sexagesima Sunday)
In the cymbals and dances, * upon the strings and pipe, O praise the Lord.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 165 (Sexagesima Sunday)
When much people * were gathered together to Jesus, and were come to him out of every city, he spake by a parable: A sower went out to sow his seed.

[magnificat-antiphon]
Jesus said to His disciples: * Unto you it is given to know the mysteries of the kingdom of God, but to others in parables.

[commemoration-antiphon]
The Lord said unto Noah: * The end of all flesh is come before Me, make thee an ark of planed timber, that seed of all flesh may be saved therein.

[commemoration-versicle]
V. Lord, thou hast been our refuge.
R. From generation to generation.

[collect]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 166 (Sexagesima Sunday)
O God, who seest that we put not our trust in anything that we do; mercifully grant that by the protection of the Teacher of the Gentiles we may be defended against all adversity.

[chapter-lauds]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 162 (Sexagesima Sunday); draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 214 (Saturday before Sexagesima)
!2 Cor 11:19-20
Brethren: Ye suffer fools gladly, seeing ye yourselves are wise. For ye suffer, if a man bring you into bondage, if a man devour you, if a man take of you, if a man exalt himself, if a man smite you on the face.
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-first]
@use proper/sexagesima/chapter-lauds

[chapter-vespers]
# "Chapter as above at Lauds" — Monastic Diurnal p. 240 (At Vespers). The
# hymn, short responsory and versicle are NOT proper here: the same rubric
# sends them "as in the Psalter, p. 114".
@use proper/sexagesima/chapter-lauds

[versicle-lauds]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 164 (Sexagesima Sunday)
V. Lord, thou hast been our refuge.
R. From one generation to another.

[magnificat-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 240 (Week of Sexagesima, Monday)
If ye seek * the summit of true honour, hasten to yon heavenly country with what speed ye may.

[magnificat-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 240 (Week of Sexagesima, Tuesday)
The seed * is the word of God, but Christ is the Sower: every one that heareth him shall abide for ever.

[magnificat-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 240 (Week of Sexagesima, Wednesday)
But that * which fell on the good ground are they which in an honest and good heart receive the word; and bring forth fruit with patience.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: Antiphons on the Magnificat for Saturday Vespers Throughout the Year.docx, p. 1 (Dixit Dominus ad Noe)
The Lord * said unto Noah: The end of all flesh is come before me: Make thee an ark of gopher wood, that therein the seed of all flesh may be saved.
