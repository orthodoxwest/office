# SOURCE: diurnal p. 627
Almighty everlasting God, author of all virtue and lover of virginity: grant, we beseech thee, that we may be commended by the merits of holy Frideswide, thy Virgin, which were well-pleasing unto thee; and whose life of chastity deserved to win thy favour.
