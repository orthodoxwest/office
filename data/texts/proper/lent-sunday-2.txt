[psalm-antiphon]
@use proper/lent-sunday-2/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 200 (Second Sunday in Lent)
Thou shalt open * my lips, O Lord, and my mouth shall shew thy praise.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 201 (Second Sunday in Lent)
The right hand of the Lord * bringeth mighty things to pass: the right hand of the Lord hath the pre-eminence.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 203 (Second Sunday in Lent)
Thou hast been * my helper, O my God.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 204 (Second Sunday in Lent)
Let us sing the song * of the three children, which they sang as they blessed the Lord in the burning fiery furnace.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 205 (Second Sunday in Lent)
He hath made them fast * for ever and ever: he hath given them a law which shall not be broken.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 209 (Second Sunday in Lent)
And Jesus taketh his disciples, * and goeth up into a mountain, and was transfigured before them.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 254, 256 (II Sunday in Lent, At I and II Vespers)
Tell the vision * which ye have seen to no man, until the Son of Man be risen again from the dead.

[collect]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds/Vespers books, pp. 210, 141 (Second Sunday in Lent)
Almighty God, who seest that we have no power of ourselves to help ourselves; keep us both outwardly in our bodies, and inwardly in our souls; that we may be defended from all adversities which may happen to the body, and from all evil thoughts which may assault and hurt the soul.

[chapter-lauds]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 255 (II Sunday in Lent, At Lauds)
!1 Thess 4:1
We beseech you, brethren, and exhort you by the Lord Jesus, that as ye have received of us how ye ought to walk and to please God, * so ye would abound more and more.
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-sext]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 255–256 (II Sunday in Lent, At Sext)
!1 Thess 4:3-4
For this is the will of God, even your sanctification, that ye should abstain from fornication: * that every one of you should know how to possess his vessel in sanctification and honour.
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-none]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 256 (II Sunday in Lent, At None)
!1 Thess 4:7
For God hath not called us unto uncleanness, * but unto holiness in Christ Jesus our Lord.
R. Thanks be to God.

[benedictus-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 257 (II Week in Lent, Monday)
I am the Beginning, * even I that speak unto you.

[collect-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 257 (II Week in Lent, Monday, Collect at Lauds)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God; that thy servants who abstain from food in mortification of the flesh, may fast from sin by following after righteousness.

[magnificat-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 257 (II Week in Lent, Monday)
He that sent me * is with me; the Father hath not left me alone; for I do always those things that please him.

[collect-vespers-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 257 (II Week in Lent, Monday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Assist us mercifully, Almighty God, in these our supplications; and grant that we, to whom thou hast given a full assurance of hope in thy goodness, may receive the fruit of thy wonted loving-kindness.

[benedictus-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 257 (II Week in Lent, Tuesday)
One is your Master, * which is in heaven, even Christ the Lord.

[collect-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 257 (II Week in Lent, Tuesday, Collect at Lauds)
Perfect in us, O merciful Lord, we beseech thee, the supporting strength bestowed in this holy observance: that as by thine appointment, we know what we ought to do, so by thine effectual working we may fulfil the same.

[magnificat-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 257 (II Week in Lent, Tuesday)
But all ye are brethren; * and call no man your father upon earth; for one is your Father, which is in heaven: neither be ye called masters; for one is your Master, even Christ.

[collect-vespers-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 257, 258 (II Week in Lent, Tuesday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Be favourable to our supplications, O Lord, and relieve the weakness of our souls: that receiving thy forgiveness, we may alway rejoice in thy blessing.

[benedictus-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 258 (II Week in Lent, Wednesday)
Behold, we go up * to Jerusalem: and the Son of man shall be betrayed to be crucified.

[collect-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 258 (II Week in Lent, Wednesday, Collect at Lauds)
We beseech thee, O Lord, favourably to behold thy people: and grant that those whom thou hast bidden to abstain from carnal food, may also cease from all hurtful vices.

[magnificat-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 258 (II Week in Lent, Wednesday)
For he shall be delivered * unto the Gentiles, to mock, and to scourge, and to crucify him.

[collect-vespers-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 258 (II Week in Lent, Wednesday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
O God, the restorer and lover of innocence, direct unto thyself the hearts of thy servants: that being enkindled with the ardour of thy spirit, they may be found both steadfast in faith, and fruitful in good works.

[benedictus-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 258 (II Week in Lent, Thursday)
Son, remember * that thou in thy lifetime receivedst thy good things, and likewise Lazarus evil things.

[collect-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 258 (II Week in Lent, Thursday, Collect at Lauds)
Grant us, O Lord, we beseech thee, the help of thy grace: that we, being duly given to fasting and prayer, may be delivered from the enemies of both soul and body.

[magnificat-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 258 (II Week in Lent, Thursday)
That rich man * who refused to Lazarus a crumb of bread, desired a drop of water.

[collect-vespers-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 258 (II Week in Lent, Thursday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Assist thy servants, O Lord; and at their petition bestow upon them thy continual loving-kindness: whereby thou mayest gather and restore those who glory in thee their Creator and Ruler, and mayest preserve those whom thou dost restore.

[benedictus-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 259 (II Week in Lent, Friday)
He will miserably destroy * those wicked men: and will let out his vineyard unto other husbandmen, which shall render him the fruits in their seasons.

[collect-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 259 (II Week in Lent, Friday, Collect at Lauds)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that being cleansed by the hallowed fast, thou mayest bring us with pure minds to the holy feast which is drawing nigh.

[magnificat-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 259 (II Week in Lent, Friday)
When they sought to lay hands on him, * they feared the multitude, because they took him for a prophet.

[collect-vespers-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 259 (II Week in Lent, Friday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Grant unto thy people, we beseech thee, O Lord, health of mind and body: that cleaving to good works, they may ever deserve to be defended by thy mighty protection.

[benedictus-antiphon-saturday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 259 (II Week in Lent, Saturday)
I will go * to my father, and will say unto him: Father, make me as one of thy hired servants.

[collect-saturday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 259 (II Week in Lent, Saturday, Collect at Lauds)
Grant, we beseech thee, O Lord, a healthful effect to our fasts: that the chastisement of the flesh which we have taken upon us may avail to the strengthening of our souls.
