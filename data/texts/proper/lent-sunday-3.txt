O be favourable and gracious * unto Sion: build thou, O Lord, the walls of Jerusalem.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 212 (Third Sunday in Lent)
The Lord * is on my side: I will not fear what man doeth unto me.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 214 (Third Sunday in Lent)
My lips shall praise thee * as long as I live, O my God.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 215 (Third Sunday in Lent)
The fire forgat his power and virtue * that thy children might be delivered unharmed.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 216 (Third Sunday in Lent)
O ye sun and moon, * praise ye the Lord: for his Name only is excellent.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 220 (Third Sunday in Lent)
When a strong man armed * keepeth his palace, all his goods are in peace.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 261 (III Sunday in Lent, At II Vespers)
A certain woman * of the company lifted up her voice and cried, Blessed is the womb that bare thee, and the paps which thou hast sucked. But Jesus answered, Yea, rather, blessed are they that hear the word of God, and keep it.

[collect]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 260
We beseech thee, Almighty God, look upon the hearty desires of thy humble servants: and stretch forth the right hand of thy Majesty, to be our defence against all our enemies.

[chapter-lauds]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 260 (III Sunday in Lent, At Lauds)
!Eph 5:1-2
Brethren: Be ye therefore followers of God, as dear children; and walk in love, as Christ also hath loved us, * and hath given himself for us, an offering and a sacrifice to God for a sweet-smelling savour.
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-sext]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 261 (III Sunday in Lent, At Sext)
!Eph 5:5
For this ye know, that no whoremonger, nor unclean person, nor covetous man, who is an idolater, * hath any inheritance in the kingdom of Christ and of God.
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-none]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 261 (III Sunday in Lent, At None)
!Eph 5:8-9
For ye were sometimes darkness, but now are ye light in the Lord: * walk as children of light; for the fruit of the Spirit is in all goodness and righteousness and truth.
R. Thanks be to God.

[benedictus-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 261 (III Week in Lent, Monday)
Verily I say unto you, * No prophet is accepted in his own country.

[collect-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 261 (III Week in Lent, Monday, Collect at Lauds)
We beseech thee, O merciful Lord, pour thy grace into our hearts: that like as we do abstain from bodily food, so we may restrain our senses from every harmful indulgence.

[magnificat-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 262 (III Week in Lent, Monday)
But Jesus, passing * through the midst of them, went his way.

[collect-vespers-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 262 (III Week in Lent, Monday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Let thy merciful kindness, O Lord, be present to help us: that we may ever deserve to be rescued from the imminent perils of our sins by thy protection, and also to be saved by thy deliverance.

[benedictus-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 262 (III Week in Lent, Tuesday)
If two of you * shall agree on earth as touching anything that they shall ask, it shall be done for them of my Father, saith the Lord.

[collect-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 262 (III Week in Lent, Tuesday, Collect at Lauds)
Hear us, O Almighty and merciful God: and of thy favour bestow upon us the gift of wholesome self-restraint.

[magnificat-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 262 (III Week in Lent, Tuesday)
Where two or three * are gathered together in my Name, there am I in the midst of them, saith the Lord.

[collect-vespers-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 262 (III Week in Lent, Tuesday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Defend us, O Lord, by thy protection: and keep us evermore from all iniquity.

[benedictus-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 262 (III Week in Lent, Wednesday)
Hearken, * and understand ye the traditions which the Lord hath given you.

[collect-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 262 (III Week in Lent, Wednesday, Collect at Lauds)
Grant unto us, we beseech thee, O Lord: that by the wholesome discipline of fasting, and by abstaining from all hurtful offences, we may the more easily obtain thy merciful forgiveness.

[magnificat-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 262 (III Week in Lent, Wednesday)
To eat with unwashen hands * defileth not a man.

[collect-vespers-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 262, 263 (III Week in Lent, Wednesday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that we who seek the grace of thy protection, may be delivered from all evils and serve thee with a quiet mind.

[benedictus-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 263 (III Week in Lent, Thursday)
And devils also * came out of many, crying out and saying: Thou art Christ the Son of God. And he, rebuking them, suffered them not to speak: for they knew that he was Christ.

[collect-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 263 (III Week in Lent, Thursday, Collect at Lauds (the Benedictine collect, printed before the Sarum Collect))
May the blessed solemnity of thy Saints Cosmas and Damian magnify thee, O Lord: in which, by thy ineffable providence, thou hast both granted them eternal glory, and us the benefit of their help.

[magnificat-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 263 (III Week in Lent, Thursday)
Whosoever had any sick, * they brought them unto Jesus; and they were healed.

[collect-vespers-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 263 (III Week in Lent, Thursday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Let thy heavenly favour, we beseech thee, O Lord, increase the people that serveth thee: and make them ever to keep thy commandments.

[benedictus-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 263 (III Week in Lent, Friday)
Whosoever drinketh * of the water that I shall give him shall never thirst.

[collect-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 263 (III Week in Lent, Friday, Collect at Lauds)
Lord, we pray thee, let thy gracious favour attend upon our fast: that as in the body we abstain from food, so likewise in the spirit we may fast from sin.

[magnificat-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 263 (III Week in Lent, Friday)
Sir, I perceive * that thou art a prophet. Our fathers worshipped in this mountain.

[collect-vespers-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 264 (III Week in Lent, Friday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that we who put our trust in thy protection may by thy help overcome all things that withstand us.

[benedictus-antiphon-saturday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 264 (III Week in Lent, Saturday)
But Jesus stooped down, * and wrote on the ground: If any is without sin, let him cast a stone at her.

[collect-saturday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 264 (III Week in Lent, Saturday, Collect at Lauds)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that they who abstain from food in mortification of the flesh, may fast from sin by following after righteousness.

# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 259 (III Sunday in Lent, At I Vespers)
But the father * said to his servants, Bring forth the best robe and put it on him; and put a ring on his hand, and shoes on his feet.
