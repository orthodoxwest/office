# SOURCE: Antiphons on the Benedictus for Sunday Lauds Throughout the Year.docx, p. 8 (Cum intraret)
As Jesus went * into the house of one of the chief Pharisees to eat bread on the sabbath day, behold there was a certain man before him which had the dropsy; and he took him, and healed him, and let him go.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 437–438 (XVI Sunday after Pentecost)
When thou art bidden to * a wedding, sit down in the lowest place; that he that bade thee may say unto thee, Friend, go up higher; then shalt thou have worship in the presence of them that sit at meat with thee, alleluia.
