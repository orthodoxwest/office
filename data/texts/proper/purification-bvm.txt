[psalm-antiphon-1-first-vespers]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 270 (I Vespers, Purification BVM)
O wondrous interchange! * The Creator of mankind, taking upon him a living body, vouchsafed to be born of a Virgin: and proceeding forth as Man, without seed, hath made us partakers of his Divinity.

[psalm-antiphon-2-first-vespers]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 271 (I Vespers, Purification BVM)
When thou wast born * ineffably of a Virgin, then were the Scriptures fulfilled: thou camest down like the rain into a fleece of wool, to bring salvation unto all mankind: we praise thee, O our God.

[psalm-antiphon-3-first-vespers]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 272 (I Vespers, Purification BVM)
In the bush * which Moses saw unconsumed, we acknowledge the preservation of thy glorious virginity: holy Mother of God, intercede for thy children.

[psalm-antiphon-4-first-vespers]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 273 (I Vespers, Purification BVM)
# NOTE: the source suppresses the final alleluia when February 2 falls after Septuagesima; the current text resolver has no inline date-conditional antiphon form.
Lo, Mary * hath brought forth to us the Saviour; whom when John beheld, he cried saying: Behold the Lamb of God, which taketh away the sin of the world, alleluia.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 470 (Purification BVM, At Lauds)
And when the parents * brought in the Child Jesus, then Simeon took him up in his arms, and blessed God, saying: Lord, now lettest thou thy servant depart in peace.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 471 (Purification BVM, At II Vespers)
To-day * the blessed Virgin Mary presented the Child Jesus in the temple; and Simeon, filled with the Holy Spirit, received him into his arms, and blessed God for ever.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 469 (Purification BVM, At I Vespers)
The ancient * carried the Infant, but the Infant governed the ancient: he whom a Virgin bare, and after bearing, remained virgin, the same was worshipped by her who bare him.
