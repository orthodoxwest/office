[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 70* (Office of Saint Mary on Saturday, II After Christmas, At Lauds)
A great and wondrous mystery * is made known to us this day; a new thing is wrought in both natures: God is made man; that which was, remained, and that which was not, he assumed; suffering no confusion, nor yet division.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 70* (Office of Saint Mary on Saturday, II After Christmas, At Vespers)
Great * is the mystery of the inheritance: the womb of her that knew not man is become the temple of the Godhead; by taking flesh of her, he was no way defiled: all the nations shall gather, saying: Glory be to thee, O Lord.
