[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 459 (St Agnes, At I Vespers)
Blessed Agnes, * in the midst of the flames, stretched out her hands and prayed: I call on thee, O Father transcendent, august, and dread; for by thy holy Son's protection I have escaped the threats of an impious tyrant, and passed unscathed through the foulness of fleshly pollution: and behold, I come to thee, whom I have loved, whom I have sought, whom I have alway desired.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 460 (St Agnes, At Lauds)
Lo, that which I desired, * now I see; that for which I hoped, I now possess; I am united in heaven unto him, whom on earth I loved with a perfect devotion.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 461 (St Agnes, At II Vespers)
While the blessed Agnes * was standing in the midst of the flames, she stretched out her hands, and prayed unto God: Almighty Lord, worthy of all adoration, fear, and worship: I bless thy holy Name, and I glorify thee for ever and ever.

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 460 (St Agnes, At Lauds)
When Agnes entered * the place of shame, she found the Angel of the Lord ready beforehand.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 460 (St Agnes, At Lauds)
For I have with me * an Angel of the Lord, the Guardian of my body.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 460 (St Agnes, At Lauds)
With his ring * hath my Lord Jesus Christ betrothed me, and as his bride hath he crowned me.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 460 (St Agnes, At Lauds)
I bless thee, * O Father of my Lord Jesus Christ: for by thy Son the fire is quenched round about thine handmaid.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 460 (St Agnes, At Lauds)
Rejoice with me * and be exceeding glad, because among all these I have taken a throne of glory.

I will thank thee, O Lord and King, and praise thee, O God my Saviour: I do give praise unto thy Name: for thou art my defender and helper, * and hast preserved my body from destruction.

[chapter-sext]

!Sir 51:3
Thou hast delivered me, according to the multitude of thy mercies and greatness of thy Name, from the teeth of them that were ready to devour me, and out of the hands of such as sought after my life, * and from the manifold afflictions which I had.
R. Thanks be to God.
