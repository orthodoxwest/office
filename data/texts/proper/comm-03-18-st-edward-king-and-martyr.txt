[commemoration-collect]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal p. 537 (June 20, Translation of St Edward, K.M., Collect); the 2026 ordo commemorates Edward on 18 March with "Col. 537"
O God, who reignest in glory over an eternal kingdom, mercifully regard thy family who celebrate the memory of blessed King Edward thy Martyr: and grant that by his merits and intercession, those who rejoice in his triumph may also attain to his everlasting rewards.
