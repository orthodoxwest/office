[commemoration-collect]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 549
O God, who in giving the keys of the kingdom of heaven to thy blessed Apostle Peter, didst confer upon him the priestly power of binding and loosing: grant that by the help of his intercession, we may be delivered from the bonds of our sins.
