# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 614 (January 19, Ss Marius, Martha, Audifax and Abachum, Collect)
Graciously hear thy people, O Lord, who call upon thee with the advocacy of thy Saints: and grant us both to rejoice in peace in this temporal life; and to find succour unto life everlasting.
