# SOURCE: supplemental-collects-2026-11.pdf ("Collects for Supplemental Offices"), PDF p. 5 (June 28, St Leo II)
# The sheet prints "to by thine under-shepherd"; read "be", as in its collects for Ss Cletus and Marcellinus, St Zephyrinus and St Mark.
Be merciful to the people of thy flock, O Lord, eternal Shepherd and Bishop of the souls of men: and keep us in thy continual protection; at the intercession of thy Holy Father Leo whom thou didst raise up in thy Church to be thine under-shepherd.
