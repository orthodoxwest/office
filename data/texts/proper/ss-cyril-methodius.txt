[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Sancti/07-07.txt [Ant 1] — check against diurnal
# The diurnal prints only a memorial collect (p. 559); no gospel ants.
How beautiful * are the feet of them that preach the gospel of peace, and bring glad tidings of good things, that say unto Sion thy God reigneth.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Sancti/07-07.txt [Ant 2] — check against diurnal
In holiness * and righteousness before Him all the days of their life did they serve the Lord, and therefore the Lord God of Israel hath clothed them with a robe of glory.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Sancti/07-07.txt [Ant 3] — check against diurnal
These are * the holy men become the friends of God and made glorious by the preaching of divine truth; their tongues have become the keys of heaven.

# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 559 (Ss. Cyril and Methodius)
Almighty everlasting God, who by thy blessed Bishops and Confessors Cyril and Methodius didst vouchsafe to call the Slavic peoples to the knowledge of thy Name: grant that we who glory in their festival may be joined unto their fellowship.
