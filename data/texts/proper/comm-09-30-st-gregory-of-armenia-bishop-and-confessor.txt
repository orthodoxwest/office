# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 784 (September 30, Commemoration of St Gregory of Armenia, Collect)
O God, who, through thy blessed Bishop and Confessor Gregory, didst grant the people and King of Armenia to receive the light of true faith: likewise grant unto thy Church to rejoice in such mighty triumphs, and, by the merits and intercession of the same, to be succoured before thee.
