# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 780 (September 26, Ss Cyprian and Justina, Collect)
O Lord, let the protection of thy blessed Martyrs, Cyprian and Justina, continually defend us: forasmuch as thou failest not to look with mercy on those to whom thou dost grant the succour of thy assistance.
