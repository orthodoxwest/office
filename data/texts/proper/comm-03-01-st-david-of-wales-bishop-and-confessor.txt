# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal p. 482 (March 1, St David, B.C., Collect); the 2026 ordo cites Col. 482
Grant unto us, Almighty God, that the devout intercession of blessed David, thy Confessor and Bishop, may protect us: and that while we keep his feast day, we may follow his firmness in defending the Catholic Faith.
