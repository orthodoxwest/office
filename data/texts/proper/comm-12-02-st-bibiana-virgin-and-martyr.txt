# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 598 (December 2, St Bibiana, Collect)
O God, the giver of all good gifts, who in thy handmaid Bibiana didst unite the palm of martyrdom with the flower of virginity: unite by her intercession our hearts in charity with thee; that all perils being done away, we may attain unto everlasting rewards.
