# SOURCE: diurnal p. 483
Grant us, we beseech thee, O Lord our God, to venerate with unceasing devotion the triumphs of Perpetua and Felicitas, thy holy Martyrs: that we who are not able to celebrate their victories worthily in thought, at least may often recall them in humble obedience.
