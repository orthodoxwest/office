[psalm-antiphon]
@use proper/septuagesima/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 143 (Septuagesima Sunday)
Have mercy, O God, upon me, * and cleanse me from my wickedness; for against thee only have I sinned.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 144 (Septuagesima Sunday)
I will thank thee, * for thou hast heard me.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 146 (Septuagesima Sunday)
O God, thou art my God, * early will I seek thee, because thou hast been my helper.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 147 (Septuagesima Sunday)
Blessed art thou * in the firmament of heaven: and worthy to be praised for ever, O our God.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 148 (Septuagesima Sunday)
O praise * the Lord of heaven.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 153 (Septuagesima Sunday)
The kingdom * of heaven is like unto a man that is an householder, which went out early in the morning to hire labourers into his vineyard, saith the Lord.

[magnificat-antiphon]
The householder saith unto his labourers: * Why stand ye here all the day idle? But they answered and said: Because no man hath hired us. Go ye also into my vineyard, and whatsoever is right, that will I give you.

[commemoration-antiphon]
The Lord said unto Adam: * Of the tree which is in the midst of the garden, thou shalt not eat; in the hour that thou eatest thereof, thou shalt surely die.

[commemoration-versicle]
V. O Lord, have mercy upon us.
R. For we have sinned against thee.

[collect]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 154 (Septuagesima Sunday)
O Lord, we beseech thee favourably to hear the prayers of thy people; that we, who are justly punished for our offences, may be mercifully delivered by thy goodness, for the glory of thy Name.

[chapter-lauds]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 150 (Septuagesima Sunday); draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 214 (Saturday before Septuagesima)
!1 Cor 9:24
Brethren: Know ye not that they which run a race run all, but one receiveth the prize? So run, that ye may obtain.
R. Thanks be to God.

@use proper/septuagesima/chapter-lauds

[versicle-lauds]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 152 (Septuagesima Sunday)
V. Lord, thou hast been our refuge.
R. From one generation to another.

[chapter-sext]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 236 (Septuagesima Sunday, At Sext)
!1 Cor 9:25
And every man that striveth for the mastery is temperate in all things. Now they do it to obtain a corruptible crown; * but we an incorruptible.
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-none]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 237 (Septuagesima Sunday, At None)
!1 Cor 10:4-5
For they drank of that spiritual Rock which followed them: and that Rock was Christ. * But with some of them God was not well pleased.
R. Thanks be to God.

[magnificat-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 237 (Week of Septuagesima, Monday)
These last * have wrought but one hour, and thou hast made them equal unto us, which have borne the burden and heat of the day.

[magnificat-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 237 (Week of Septuagesima, Tuesday)
The householder answered and said, * Friend, I do thee no wrong: didst not thou agree with me for a penny? Take that thine is, and go thy way.

[magnificat-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 238 (Week of Septuagesima, Wednesday)
Take that thine is * and go thy way: because I am good, saith the Lord.

[magnificat-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 238 (Week of Septuagesima, Thursday)
Is it not lawful * for me to do what I will? Is thine eye evil, because I am good? saith the Lord.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: Antiphons on the Magnificat for Saturday Vespers Throughout the Year.docx, p. 1 (Dixit Dominus)
The Lord said * unto Adam, Of the tree which is in the midst of the garden thou shalt not eat: for in the day that thou eatest thereof, thou shalt surely die.
