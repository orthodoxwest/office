# SOURCE: diurnal p. 454
O God, who didst adorn blessed Titus, thy Bishop and Confessor, with the signs of an Apostle: grant that through his merits and intercession, we may live soberly, righteously, and godly, in this present world; and be made meet hereafter to come unto the heavenly country.
