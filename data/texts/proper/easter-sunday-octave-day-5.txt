[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 368–369 (Thursday in Easter Week)
Mary stood * at the sepulchre weeping, and seeth two Angels in white, sitting, and the napkin that was about the head of Jesus, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 369 (Thursday in Easter Week)
They have taken * away my Lord, and I know not where they have laid him. If thou have borne him hence, tell me, alleluia: and I will take him away, alleluia.
