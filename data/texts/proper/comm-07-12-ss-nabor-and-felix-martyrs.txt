# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 721 (July 12, Ss Nabor and Felix, Collect)
Grant, we beseech thee, O Lord: that as the birthday of thy holy Martyrs, Nabor and Felix, faileth not to return for our observance; so they may continually assist us by their prayers.
