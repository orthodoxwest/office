# SOURCE: supplemental-collects-2026-11.pdf ("Collects for Supplemental Offices"), PDF p. 4 (May 24, St Vincent of Lerins)
O God, who didst raise up thy blessed Confessor Vincent to proclaim the rule of faith: grant that all thy people may stedfastly witness to the fulness of the truth; that the same may be received everywhere, always, and by all, even as thou hast taught us through thy Son, Jesus Christ our Lord.
