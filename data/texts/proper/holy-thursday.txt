[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 305
Mayest thou * be justified, O Lord, in thy saying, and clear when thou art judged.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 307
The Lord * was led as a lamb to the slaughter: and he opened not his mouth.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 308
My heart * within me is broken: and trembling maketh all my bones to shake.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 309
Thou dost encourage us * by thy might, O Lord, and by thy holy refreshment.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 311
He was offered * because he willed it: and himself hath borne our iniquities.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 311
Now he that betrayed him * gave them a sign, saying: Whomsoever I shall kiss, that same is he; hold him fast.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 315
As they were eating, * Jesus took bread, and blessed it, and brake it, and gave it to his disciples.

[commemoration-antiphon]
I will take the cup of salvation; * and call upon the Name of the Lord.

[commemoration-versicle]
V. Mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted;
R. Which did eat of my bread, hath lifted up his heel against me.
