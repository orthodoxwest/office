@use psalmody/vespers/ferial

[psalm-antiphon]
@use proper/holy-thursday/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
O Lord, Thou shalt be justified * when Thou speakest, and be clear when Thou art judged.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
The Lord was brought as a lamb * to the slaughter, and He opened not His mouth.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
Mine heart is broken within me * all my bones tremble.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
O Lord, Thou hast spoken unto us * in thy strength, and in thy Holy Banquet.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
He was offered up because He willed it * and He bore our sins.

[benedictus-antiphon]
Now he that betrayed Him * gave them a sign, saying: Whomsoever I shall kiss, That Same is He: hold Him fast.

[magnificat-antiphon]
And, as they were eating * Jesus took bread, and blessed, and broke it, and gave to His disciples.

[commemoration-antiphon]
I will take the cup of salvation; * and call upon the Name of the Lord.

[commemoration-versicle]
V. Mine own familiar friend, in whom I trusted;
R. Which did eat of my bread, hath lifted up his heel against me.

[collect]
O God, from whom Judas received the punishment of his guilt, and the thief the reward of his confession: grant unto us the full effect of thy clemency; that as in his Passion our Lord Jesus Christ gave to each a recompense meet for his deserts, so having taken away our old sins, he may bestow on us the grace of his resurrection.

# Vespers, p. 315, which adds the versicle ("The Chapter, Short R., Hymn, and V.
# are not said during this Triduum"). Lauds keeps a proper versicle, printed for
# each day of the Triduum; Vespers has none.
