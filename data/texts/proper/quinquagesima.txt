[psalm-antiphon]
@use proper/quinquagesima/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 167 (Quinquagesima Sunday)
According to the multitude * of thy mercies, O Lord, do away mine offences.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 168 (Quinquagesima Sunday)
Thou art my God, * and I will thank thee: thou art my God, and I will praise thee.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 170 (Quinquagesima Sunday)
Early will I seek thee, * O God, that I may behold thy power.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 171 (Quinquagesima Sunday)
Sing a hymn, * and magnify him for ever.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 172 (Quinquagesima Sunday)
All ye Angels of his, * O praise the Lord of heaven.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 177 (Quinquagesima Sunday)
Behold, we go up * to Jerusalem, and all things that are written concerning the Son of man shall be accomplished: for he shall be delivered unto the Gentiles, and shall be mocked, and spitted on; and they shall scourge him, and put him to death; and the third day he shall rise again.

[magnificat-antiphon]
And Jesus stood, * and commanded the blind man to be brought unto Him, and He asked him, saying: What wilt thou that I shall do unto thee? Lord, that I may receive my sight. And Jesus said unto him: Receive thy sight, thy faith hath saved thee. And immediately he received his sight, and followed Him, glorifying God.

[commemoration-antiphon]
The father of our faith, * the mighty Abraham, offered a burnt offering on the altar for his son.

[commemoration-versicle]
V. O Lord, have mercy upon us.
R. For we have sinned against thee.

[collect]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 178 (Quinquagesima Sunday)
Graciously hear our prayers, we beseech thee, O Lord: that we, being loosed from the chain of our sins, may be kept from all things that may hurt us.

[chapter-lauds]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 174 (Quinquagesima Sunday); draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 214 (Saturday before Quinquagesima)
!1 Cor 13:1
Brethren: Though I speak with the tongues of men and of angels, and have not charity, I am become as sounding brass, or a tinkling cymbal.
R. Thanks be to God.

[versicle-lauds]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 176 (Quinquagesima Sunday)
V. Lord, thou hast been our refuge.
R. From one generation to another.

[magnificat-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Tempora/Quadp3-1 [Ant 3] — check against diurnal
And they that went before * rebuked him, that he should hold his peace. But he cried out so much the more: "Have mercy on me, thou Son of David."

[magnificat-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Tempora/Quadp3-2 [Ant 3] — check against diurnal
Have mercy on me, * thou Son of David. What wouldst thou that I should do unto thee? Lord, that I may receive my sight.

[benedictus-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Tempora/Quadp3-3 [Ant 2] — check against diurnal
When ye fast, * be not, as the hypocrites, of a sad countenance.

[magnificat-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Tempora/Quadp3-3 [Ant 3] — check against diurnal
Lay up for yourselves treasures in heaven, * where neither moth nor rust doth corrupt.

[benedictus-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Tempora/Quadp3-4 [Ant 2] — check against diurnal
Lord, * my servant lieth at home sick of the palsy, and is grieviously tormented. Amen, I say unto thee, I will come and heal him.

[magnificat-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Tempora/Quadp3-4 [Ant 3] — check against diurnal
O Lord, * I am not worthy that thou shouldst enter under my roof: but only say the word, and my servant shall be healed.

[benedictus-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Tempora/Quadp3-5 [Ant 2] — check against diurnal
When thou dost * alms, let not thy left hand know what thy right hand doth.

[magnificat-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium TemporaM/Quadp3-5 [Ant 3] — check against diurnal
But thou when thou shalt pray, * enter into thy chamber, and having shut the door, pray to thy Father.

[benedictus-antiphon-saturday]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Tempora/Quadp3-6 [Ant 2] — check against diurnal
For they seek me * from day to day, and desire to know my ways.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: Antiphons on the Magnificat for Saturday Vespers Throughout the Year.docx, p. 1 (Pater fidei)
Mighty Abraham, * the father of our faith, offered a burnt offering upon the altar, instead of his son.
