# SOURCE: supplemental-collects-2026-11.pdf ("Collects for Supplemental Offices"), PDF p. 10 (October 25, Ss Chrysanthus and Daria)
We beseech thee, O Lord, that we may in such wise be assisted by the prayers of thy blessed Martyrs, Chrysanthus and Daria: that, like as we do here render them our outward honour; so we may ever feel the succour of their loving-kindness.
