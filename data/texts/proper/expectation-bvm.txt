[collect]
# SOURCE: Expectation BVM MD Propers.pdf, p. 1 (At Lauds, Collect; prints "who believer her", corrected to "believe")
O God, who didst ordain that thy Word should take flesh in the womb of the blessed Virgin Mary, at the message of an Angel: grant that we thy suppliants, who believe her to be indeed the Mother of God, may be aided by her intercessions before thee.
