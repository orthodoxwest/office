# SOURCE: supplemental-collects-2026-11.pdf ("Collects for Supplemental Offices"), PDF p. 2 (April 14, Ss Tiburtius, Valerian and Maximus)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that we who keep this feast of thy blessed Martyrs, Tiburtius, Valerian, and Maximus; may likewise follow the example of their virtues.
