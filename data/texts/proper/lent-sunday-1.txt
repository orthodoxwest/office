[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 189 (First Sunday in Lent)
Make me * a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 190 (First Sunday in Lent)
Help me now, * O Lord: O Lord, send us now prosperity.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 192 (First Sunday in Lent)
As long as I live, O Lord, * will I magnify thee on this manner, and lift up my hands in thy Name.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 193 (First Sunday in Lent)
In the spirit of humility * and with a contrite heart, let us be accepted by thee, O Lord: and so let our sacrifice be offered as to be accepted by thee to-day, and be pleasing in thy sight, O Lord our God.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 194 (First Sunday in Lent)
O praise God, * all ye heavens, and all ye waters.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 198 (First Sunday in Lent)
Then was Jesus * led up of the Spirit into the wilderness to be tempted of the devil: and when he had fasted forty days and forty nights, he was afterward an hungred.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 249–250 (I Sunday in Lent, At II Vespers)
Behold, now * is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation: let us therefore in all things approve ourselves as the ministers of God, in much patience, in watchings, in fastings, and by love unfeigned.

[commemoration-antiphon]

[commemoration-versicle]
V. I will bless the Lord at all times.
R. His praise shall continually be in my mouth.

[collect]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 248 (I Sunday in Lent)
O God, who dost purify thy Church by the yearly observance of the Lenten fast: grant unto this thy family, that what they endeavour to obtain of thee by abstinence, they may shew forth in good works.

[chapter-lauds]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 247 (I Sunday in Lent, At Lauds)
!2 Cor 6:1-2
Brethren: We beseech you also that ye receive not the grace of God in vain. For he saith, I have heard thee in a time accepted, * and in the day of salvation have I succoured thee.
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-sext]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 249 (I Sunday in Lent, At Sext)
!2 Cor 6:2-3
Behold, now is the accepted time; behold, now is the day of salvation. Giving no offence in anything, * that the ministry be not blamed.
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-none]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 249 (I Sunday in Lent, At None)
!2 Cor 6:9-10
As chastened, and not killed; as sorrowful, yet alway rejoicing; as poor, yet making many rich; * as having nothing, and yet possessing all things.
R. Thanks be to God.

[benedictus-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 252 (I Week in Lent, Monday)
Come, ye blessed * of my Father, inherit the kingdom prepared for you from the foundation of the world.

[magnificat-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 252 (I Week in Lent, Monday)
That which ye have done * unto one of the least of mine, ye have done unto me, saith the Lord.

[benedictus-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 252 (I Week in Lent, Tuesday)
And Jesus * went into the temple of God, and cast out all them that sold and bought: and overthrew the tables of the money-changers, and the seats of them that sold doves.

[magnificat-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 252 (I Week in Lent, Tuesday)
Truly it is written, * Mine house shall be called an house of prayer for all nations: but ye have made it a den of robbers. And he was daily with them, teaching in the temple.

[benedictus-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 252–253 (I Week in Lent, Ember Wednesday)
This crooked * and perverse generation seeketh after a sign: and there shall no sign be given it, but the sign of the prophet Jonas.

[magnificat-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 253 (I Week in Lent, Ember Wednesday)
For as Jonas * was three days and three nights in the whale's belly, so shall the Son of Man be in the heart of the earth.

[benedictus-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 253 (I Week in Lent, Thursday)
Jesus went thence, * and departed into the coasts of Tyre and Sidon: and behold, a woman of Canaan came out of the same coasts, and cried, saying, Have mercy on me, thou Son of David.

[magnificat-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 253 (I Week in Lent, Thursday)
O woman, * great is thy faith: be it unto thee even as thou wilt.

[benedictus-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 253 (I Week in Lent, Ember Friday)
An Angel of the Lord * went down from heaven, and troubled the water, and one was healed.

[magnificat-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 254 (I Week in Lent, Ember Friday)
He that made me whole, * the same commanded me: Take up thy bed, and walk in peace.

[benedictus-antiphon-saturday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 254 (I Week in Lent, Ember Saturday)
And Jesus taketh his disciples, * and goeth up into a mountain, and was transfigured before them.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 246–247 (I Sunday in Lent, At I Vespers)
Then shalt thou call, * and the Lord shall answer: thou shalt cry, and he shall say, Here am I.
