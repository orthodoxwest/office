[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 228 (Within the Octave of the Epiphany, IV Day)
We have seen his star * in the East, and are come with gifts to worship the Lord.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 228 (Within the Octave of the Epiphany, IV Day)
Herod inquired * of the wise men, What sign have ye seen, concerning him that is born King? We have seen a star shining, the brightness whereof enlightens the world.
