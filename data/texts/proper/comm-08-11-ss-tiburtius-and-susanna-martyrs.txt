# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 750 (August 11, Ss Tiburtius and Susanna, Collect)
O Lord, let the protection of thy holy Martyrs Tiburtius and Susanna continually defend us: forasmuch as thou failest not to look with mercy on those to whom thou dost grant the succour of their assistance.
