# St Catherine of Alexandria (November 25), Double. The 2026 ordo (pp. 118-119)
# appoints the Common of Virgin-Martyrs with "Col. (suppl.)"; the collect is the
# Vicariate Missal's proper prayer. Everything else is the Common.

[collect]
# SOURCE: Ordo-2026-Suppl-Collects.pdf, English Missal for the Laity (1933) p. 832
O God, who didst give the law to Moses on the height of Mount Sinai, and in the same place didst through thy holy Angels wondrously bestow the body of blessed Catherine, thy Virgin and Martyr: grant, we beseech thee; that by her merits and intercession we may be enabled to attain unto that mount which is Christ.
