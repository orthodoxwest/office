# SOURCE: Ordo-2026-Suppl-Collects.pdf, English Missal for the Laity (1933) p. 525
O God, who makest us glad with the yearly solemnity of blessed Agnes, thy Virgin and Martyr: grant, we beseech thee; that as we venerate her in our service, so we may follow the example of her godly conversation.
