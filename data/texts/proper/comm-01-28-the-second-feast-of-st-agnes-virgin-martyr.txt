# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 624 (January 28, St Agnes of Rome, Collect)
O God, who makest us glad with the yearly solemnity of blessed Agnes, thy Virgin and Martyress: grant, we beseech thee; that as we venerate her in our service, so we may follow the example of her godly conversation.
