# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 723 (July 18, Ss Symphorosa and her Seven Sons, Collect)
O God, who vouchsafest unto us to keep the heavenly birthday of thy holy Martyrs Symphorosa and her sons: grant, we beseech thee; that we may rejoice in the perpetual felicity of their friendship.
