[commemoration-collect]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal p. 624 (October 13, Translation of St Edward, K.C., Collect); ordo "Col. 624"
O God, who hast crowned the blessed King Edward, thy Confessor, with everlasting glory: make us, we beseech thee, so to venerate him on earth that we may reign with him hereafter in heaven.
