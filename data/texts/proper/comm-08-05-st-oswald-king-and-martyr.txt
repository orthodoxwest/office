[commemoration-collect]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal pp. 580–581 (August 5, St Oswald, K.M., Collect); ordo "Col. 580"
Almighty and everlasting God, who hast consecrated the bright and holy joy of this day by the solemnity of thy blessed Martyr Oswald: grant that our hearts may grow in thy love, that we may again honour his suffering on earth, and be assured of his intercession in heaven.
