# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF pp. 764-765 (August 30, Ss Felix and Adauctus, Collect)
O Lord, we humbly entreat thy Majesty: that, like as thou dost continually gladden us with the commemoration of thy Saints; so thou wouldest evermore defend us with their supplication.
