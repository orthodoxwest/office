[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 227 (Within the Octave of the Epiphany, II Day)
From the east * there came wise men to Bethlehem, to worship the Lord: and when they had opened their treasures, they presented unto him precious gifts: gold as to a mighty King, incense as to the true God, and myrrh to foreshew his burial, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 227–228 (Within the Octave of the Epiphany, II Day)
When the wise men saw the star * they rejoiced with exceeding great joy: and when they were come into the house, they presented unto the Lord gold, frankincense, and myrrh, alleluia.
