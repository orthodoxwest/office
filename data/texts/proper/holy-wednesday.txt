[psalm-antiphon]
@use proper/holy-wednesday/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 283
Deliver me * from blood-guiltiness, O God my God: and my tongue shall sing of thy righteousness.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 283
Reproaches * and terrors have I suffered of them: and the Lord is with me as a mighty terrible one.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 283
These also * that seek the hurt of my soul, they shall go under the earth.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 283
All mine enemies * have heard of my trouble, O Lord: they are glad that thou hast done it.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 283
Bind the nations * of the heathen with chains, O Lord; and their princes with fetters.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 283
Simon, sleepest thou? * couldest not thou watch with me one hour?

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 284
A maid said unto Peter, * Surely thou art one of them, for thy speech bewrayeth thee.

[collect]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 284
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that we, who are continually afflicted because of our transgressions, may be delivered by the Passion of thine only-begotten Son.

[collect-vespers]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 284 (Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat at Vespers); 2026 ordo
Almighty God, we beseech thee graciously to behold this thy family, for which our Lord Jesus Christ was contented to be betrayed, and given up into the hands of wicked men, and to suffer death upon the cross.
