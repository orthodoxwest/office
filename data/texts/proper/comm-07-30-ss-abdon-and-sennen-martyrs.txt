[commemoration-collect]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal p. 576 (July 30, Ss Abdon and Sennen, MM., Collect); ordo "Col. 576"
O God, who didst bestow on thy Saints, Abdon and Sennen, abundant gifts of grace whereby they came to their present glory: grant unto thy servants the forgiveness of all their sins, that through the interceding merits of thy Saints they may be worthy to be delivered from all adversities.
