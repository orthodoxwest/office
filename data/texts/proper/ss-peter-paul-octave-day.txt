# Gospel antiphons appointed within the octave and on its octave day.
# Monastic Diurnal pp. 558–560. Other appointments are reviewed separately.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 558
Glorious princes * over all the earth, as in life they loved one another, so even in death they were not divided.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: diurnal p. 558
Peter the Apostle * and Paul the Doctor of the Gentiles: these have taught us thy law, O Lord.
