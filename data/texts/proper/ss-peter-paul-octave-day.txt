[collect]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal p. 560; 2026 ordo p. 78
O God, whose right hand upheld blessed Peter lest he should sink while walking on the waves; and delivered his fellow-Apostle Paul from the depth of the sea when thrice he suffered shipwreck: mercifully hear us, and grant that by the merits of them both we may attain unto the glory of eternal life.
