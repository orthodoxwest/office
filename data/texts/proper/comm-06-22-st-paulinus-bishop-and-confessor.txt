[commemoration-collect]
# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal pp. 537–538 (June 22, St Paulinus of Nola, B.C., Collect); ordo "Col. 537"
O God, who hast promised a hundredfold reward hereafter and life everlasting to those who in this world leave all things for thy sake: mercifully grant that following closely in the footsteps of the holy Bishop Paulinus, we may be enabled to despise the things of earth and to desire only those of heaven.
