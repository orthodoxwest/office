Now when it was late * the same day, the first of the week, and the doors were shut, where the disciples were gathered together, for fear of the Jews, Jesus came and stood in the midst and said to them: Peace be to you, alleluia.

[benedictus-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 376 (I Week after the Octave of Easter, Monday)
When Jesus was risen * early the first day of the week, he appeared first to Mary Magdalene, out of whom he had cast seven devils, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 376 (I Week after the Octave of Easter, Monday)
Peace be unto you, it is I, * alleluia: be not afraid, alleluia.

[benedictus-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 376 (I Week after the Octave of Easter, Tuesday)
I go before you * into Galilee: there shall ye see me, as I said unto you, alleluia, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 376 (I Week after the Octave of Easter, Tuesday)
Reach hither thy finger, * and examine the print of the nails, alleluia: and be not faithless, but believing, alleluia.

[benedictus-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 376 (I Week after the Octave of Easter, Wednesday)
I am the true vine, * alleluia: and ye are the true branches, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 376 (I Week after the Octave of Easter, Wednesday)
Because thou hast seen me, * Thomas, thou hast believed: blessed are they that have not seen, and yet have believed, alleluia.

[benedictus-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 376 (I Week after the Octave of Easter, Thursday)
My heart burns * within me; I desire to behold my Lord: I seek, and find not where they have laid him, alleluia, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 376 (I Week after the Octave of Easter, Thursday)
I did put my finger * into the print of the nails, and thrust my hand into his side, and said, My Lord and my God, alleluia.
