# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF pp. 701-702 (June 23, St Etheldreda, Collect)
O God, who makest us glad with the yearly solemnity of blessed Etheldreda, thy Virgin: mercifully grant; that as we are enlightened by the example of her purity so we may be succoured by her prayers.
