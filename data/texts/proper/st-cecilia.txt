[psalm-antiphon]
@use proper/st-cecilia/psalm-antiphon-1

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 665 (St Cecilia, At Lauds)
The organs made melody * while Cecilia sang again to the Lord, and said: Let my heart be sound in thy statutes, that I be not ashamed.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 666 (St Cecilia, At Lauds)
Then did Valerian * find Cecilia in her chamber with an Angel, making her supplication.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 666 (St Cecilia, At Lauds)
Cecilia * thine handmaid, O Lord, ever serveth thee as zealously as a busy bee.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 666 (St Cecilia, At Lauds)
I bless thee, * O Father of my Lord Jesus Christ: for by thy Son the fire is quenched round about thine handmaid.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 666 (St Cecilia, At Lauds)
I desired * of the Lord a three days' respite, that I might hallow my dwelling as a temple.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 666 (St Cecilia, At Lauds)
When the dawn of day was breaking, * Cecilia the Martyr cried, saying: Forward, soldiers of Christ! Cast off the works of darkness, and put on the armour of light.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 666 (St Cecilia, At II Vespers)
This noble maiden * alway bare the glorious Gospel of Christ in her bosom: without ceasing, she continued in prayer and supplication, and celestial converse, night and day.

[collect]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 666 (St Cecilia, At Lauds)
O God, who makest us glad with the yearly festival of blessed Cecilia thy Virgin and Martyr: grant that we, who honour her with duteous service, may also follow the example of her good and holy life.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 665 (St Cecilia, At I Vespers)
It is secret, * O Valerian, what now I wish to tell thee: I have an Angel of God who loves me, and guards my body with exceeding zeal.
