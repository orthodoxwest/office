[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Sancti/11-22 [Ant Vespera] — check against diurnal

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Sancti/11-22 [Ant Vespera] — check against diurnal
Busy like a bee, * O Lord, did thine handmaiden Cecilia serve thee.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: divinum-officium Sancti/11-22 [Ant Vespera] — check against diurnal
I bless thee, O Father of my Lord Jesus Christ, * that through thy Son the fire is gone out round about me.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
@use proper/st-cecilia/magnificat-antiphon
[benedictus-antiphon]
As dawn was fading * into day, Cecilia cried and said: Arise, O soldiers of Christ, cast away the works of darkness, and put on the armour of light.

[magnificat-antiphon]
I have asked the Lord for three days, * that I may consecrate my house as a Church.

O God, Who year by year dost gladden thy people by the solemn feast of thy blessed Virgin and Martyr Cecilia, grant unto us, we beseech thee, not only devoutly to observe the same, but also to follow after the pattern of her godly conversation.

# SOURCE: divinum-officium Sancti/11-22.txt [Ant 1] — check against diurnal
Valerian! there is a secret * which I wish to tell thee. I have an Angel of God for a lover, and he is very jealous to keep my body.
