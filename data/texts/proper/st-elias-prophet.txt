# St Elias the Prophet (July 20), Double. Source: elias-propers-july20.pdf,
# pp. 1-3 (Monastic Diurnal-format supplement); the 2026 ordo (p. 83) takes
# "Chp &c." and the Hours from it. Psalmody is left to the engine: the
# supplement appoints Sunday Lauds psalms and the Common of Apostles at
# Vespers, but the 2026 ordo prints Monday psalms at both hours. The Prime
# "Short Lesson, Thou raisedst up a dead man, as at None" has no slot.

[collect]
# SOURCE: elias-propers-july20.pdf, p. 3 (At Lauds, Collect)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that we who believe that thou didst marvellously lift up thy Prophet Elijah in a fiery chariot, while yet in this life; at his intercession may we while still alive be raised to spiritual heights and rejoice in the resurrection of the just.

[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: elias-propers-july20.pdf, p. 2 (At Lauds, Ant. 1)
With zeal have I been zealous * for the Lord God of hosts; for the children of Israel have forsaken thy covenant.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: elias-propers-july20.pdf, p. 2 (At Lauds, Ant. 2)
How long halt ye * between two opinions? if the Lord be God, follow him.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: elias-propers-july20.pdf, p. 2 (At Lauds, Ant. 3)
If I be a man of God, * let fire come down from heaven, and consume thee and thy fifty.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: elias-propers-july20.pdf, p. 2 (At Lauds, Ant. 4)
I have not * troubled Israel; but thou, and thy father’s house, in that ye have forsaken the commandments of the Lord.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: elias-propers-july20.pdf, p. 2 (At Lauds, Ant. 5)
Thou hast slain; * moreover, thou hast taken possession: behold, I will bring evil upon thee, and I will cut down thy posterity.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: elias-propers-july20.pdf, p. 3 (At Lauds, Ben. Ant.)
Elias * was a man passable like unto us; and with prayer he prayed that it might not rain upon the earth, and it rained now for three years and six months. And he prayed again; and the heaven gave rain, and the earth brought forth her fruit.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: elias-propers-july20.pdf, p. 1 (At I Vespers, Mag. Ant.)
Behold, I will * send you Elias the Prophet, before the coming of the great and dreadful day of the Lord: and he shall turn the heart of the fathers to the children, and the heart of the children to their fathers.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: elias-propers-july20.pdf, p. 3 (At II Vespers, Mag. Ant.)
Elias took his mantle * and struck the waters, and they were divided hither and thither, and he and Eliseus passed over on dry ground; and Elias went up by a whirlwind into heaven.
