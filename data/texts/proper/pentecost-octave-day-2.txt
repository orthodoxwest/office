[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 399 (Monday within the Octave of Pentecost)
God so loved the world, * that he gave his only-begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life, alleluia.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 399 (Monday within the Octave of Pentecost)
If a man love me, * he will keep my saying, and my Father will love him: and we will come unto him, and make our abode with him, alleluia.
