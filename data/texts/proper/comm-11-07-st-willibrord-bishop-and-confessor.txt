# SOURCE: supplemental-collects-2026-11.pdf ("Collects for Supplemental Offices"), PDF p. 10 (November 7, St Willibrord)
O God, who didst vouchsafe to send thy blessed Saint Willibrord to preach thy glory to the Gentiles: we humbly pray thee; that, by his merits and intercession, we may both see and know the things which we ought to do, and by thy mercy be enabled to perform the same.
