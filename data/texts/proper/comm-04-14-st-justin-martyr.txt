# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal p. 510 (April 13, St Justin, M., Collect)
O God, who through the foolishness of the Cross didst marvellously teach blessed Justin Martyr the excellent knowledge of Jesus Christ: grant, at his intercession, that, repelling the errors which beset us, we may attain unto steadfastness in the faith.
