# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 763 (August 28, 2nd collect for St Sabina)
O God, who among the manifold works of thy power hast bestowed even upon the weakness of women the victory of martyrdom: mercifully grant; that we, who celebrate the birthday of blessed Sabina, thy Martyr, may by her example draw nearer unto thee.
