# SOURCE: Full Vicariate Missal (Completed, Rough Draft), PDF p. 811 (November 4, Ss Vitalis and Agricola, Collect)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that we, who devoutly celebrate the festival of thy holy Martyrs Vitalis and Agricola, may be aided by their intercession with thee.
