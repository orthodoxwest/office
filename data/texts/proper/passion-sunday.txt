[psalm-antiphon-1]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 233 (Passion Sunday)
O Lord, * behold my affliction: for mine enemy hath magnified himself.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 234 (Passion Sunday)
I called * upon the Lord in trouble; and the Lord heard me at large.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 236 (Passion Sunday)
O Lord, thou hast pleaded * the causes of my soul: thou hast defended my life, O Lord my God.

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 237 (Passion Sunday)
O my people, * what have I done unto thee? and wherein have I wearied thee? testify against me.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 238 (Passion Sunday)
Shall evil * be recompensed for good? for they have digged a pit for my soul.

[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 243 (Passion Sunday)
Jesus said * unto the multitude of the Jews, and to the chief priests: He that is of God heareth God's words; ye therefore hear them not, because ye are not of God.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 273 (Passion Sunday, At II Vespers)
Your father Abraham * rejoiced to see my day: and he saw it and was glad.

[commemoration-antiphon]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 270 (Passion Sunday, At I Vespers)
I am one * that bear witness of myself, and the Father that sent me beareth witness of me.

[collect]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds/Vespers books, pp. 244, 151 (Passion Sunday)
We beseech thee, Almighty God, mercifully to look upon thy people: that by thy great goodness they may be governed and preserved evermore, both in body and soul.

[chapter-lauds]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 271 (Passion Sunday, At Lauds)
!Heb 9:11-12
Brethren: Christ being come an high priest of good things to come, by a greater and more perfect tabernacle, not made with hands, that is to say, not of this building; neither by the blood of goats and calves, but by his own blood he entered in once into the holy place, * having obtained eternal redemption for us.
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-sext]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 273 (Passion Sunday, At Sext)
!Heb 9:13-14
For if the blood of bulls and of goats, and the ashes of an heifer sprinkling the unclean, sanctifieth to the purifying of the flesh; how much more shall the blood of Christ, who through the eternal Spirit offered himself without spot to God, * purge your conscience from dead works to serve the living God?
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-none]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 273 (Passion Sunday, At None)
!Heb 9:15
And for this cause he is the mediator of the new testament, that by means of death, for the redemption of the transgressions that were under the first testament, they which are called might receive the promise of eternal inheritance, * in Christ Jesus our Lord.
R. Thanks be to God.

[benedictus-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 276 (Passion Week, Monday)
In the great day of the feast, * Jesus stood and cried, saying: If any man thirst, let him come unto me and drink.

[collect-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 276 (Passion Week, Monday, Collect at Lauds)
Sanctify our fast, we beseech thee, O Lord: and mercifully bestow upon us the pardon of all our faults.

[magnificat-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 276 (Passion Week, Monday)
If any man thirst, * let him come unto me and drink: and out of his belly shall flow living water, saith the Lord.

[collect-vespers-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 276 (Passion Week, Monday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Grant unto thy people, we beseech thee, O Lord, health of mind and body: that cleaving to good works, they may ever deserve to be defended by thy mighty protection.

[benedictus-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 276 (Passion Week, Tuesday)
My time * is not yet come, but your time is alway ready.

[collect-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 276 (Passion Week, Tuesday, Collect at Lauds)
May our fasts be acceptable in thy sight, O Lord, we beseech thee: and by their atoning power, both make us worthy of thy grace, and lead us on to everlasting remedies.

[magnificat-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 276 (Passion Week, Tuesday)
Go ye up * unto this feast: I go not up yet, for my time is not yet full come.

[collect-vespers-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 276 (Passion Week, Tuesday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
We beseech thee, O Lord, give us perseverance in humble service according to thy will: that in our day the people that serveth thee may increase both in number and in merit.

[benedictus-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 276 (Passion Week, Wednesday)
My sheep * hear my voice, and I the Lord both know them, and they follow me.

[collect-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 276, 277 (Passion Week, Wednesday, Collect at Lauds)
Mercifully enlighten the hearts of thy faithful people, O God, by this fast which thou hast hallowed: and in thy loving-kindness graciously give ear to thy suppliant people unto whom thou grantest the grace of devotion.

[magnificat-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 277 (Passion Week, Wednesday)
Many good works * have I wrought among you: for which of these works do ye wish to kill me?

[collect-vespers-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 277 (Passion Week, Wednesday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Assist us mercifully, Almighty God, in these our supplications: and grant that we, to whom thou hast given a full assurance of hope in thy goodness, may receive the fruit of thy wonted loving-kindness.

[benedictus-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 277 (Passion Week, Thursday)
The Master saith, * My time is at hand; I will keep the Passover at thy house with my disciples.

[collect-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 277 (Passion Week, Thursday, Collect at Lauds)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that as the dignity of human nature hath been injured through excess, so by zeal for healing frugality it may be restored.

[magnificat-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 277 (Passion Week, Thursday)
With desire * I have desired to eat this Passover with you before I suffer.

[collect-vespers-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 277 (Passion Week, Thursday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Be favourable to thy people, O Lord, we beseech thee: and grant that they, eschewing those things which displease thee, may be the more abundantly filled with the delights of thy commandments.

[benedictus-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 277 (Passion Week, Friday)
Now the feast day of the Jews * drew nigh; and the chief priests sought how they might kill Jesus, but they feared the people.

[collect-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 277, 278 (Passion Week, Friday, Collect at Lauds)
We beseech thee, O Lord, mercifully pour thy grace into our hearts: that we who restrain ourselves from sin by voluntary chastisement, may rather suffer in time than be doomed to eternal punishment.

[magnificat-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 278 (Passion Week, Friday)
The chief priests * consulted how they might take Jesus and kill him; but they said, Not on the feast day, lest there be an uproar among the people.

[collect-vespers-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 278 (Passion Week, Friday, Collect after the Ant. on Magnificat)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that we who seek the grace of thy protection, may be delivered from all evils and serve thee with a quiet mind.

[benedictus-antiphon-saturday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 278 (Passion Week, Saturday)
O Father, glorify thou me * with thine own self with the glory which I had with thee before the world began.

[collect-saturday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 278 (Passion Week, Saturday, Collect at Lauds)
We beseech thee, O Lord, let the people dedicated unto thee advance in the spirit of godly devotion: that being disciplined by holy exercises, they may become the more pleasing unto thy Majesty as they increase in more excellent gifts.
