Then shalt thou be pleased * with the sacrifice of righteousness, if thou wilt turn thy face from my sins.

[psalm-antiphon-2]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 223 (Fourth Sunday in Lent)
It is better * to trust in the Lord, than to put any confidence in princes.

[psalm-antiphon-3]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 225 (Fourth Sunday in Lent)
Have I not thought * upon thee, O Lord, when I was waking?

[psalm-antiphon-4]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 226 (Fourth Sunday in Lent)
Mighty art thou, O Lord, * to deliver us from the hand of the strong: save and deliver us, O our God.

[psalm-antiphon-5]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds book, p. 227 (Fourth Sunday in Lent)
Kings of the earth * and all people, praise ye the Lord.

# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Lauds/Vespers books, pp. 232, 141; monastic diurnal, p. 265 (Fourth Sunday in Lent)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that we, who for our evil deeds do worthily deserve to be punished, by the comfort of thy grace may mercifully be relieved; through our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ.

[chapter-lauds]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 264–265 (IV Sunday in Lent, At Lauds)
!Gal 4:22-24
Brethren: It is written, that Abraham had two sons; the one by a bondmaid, the other by a freewoman. But he who was of the bondwoman was born after the flesh: but he of the freewoman was by promise. * Which things are an allegory.
R. Thanks be to God.

[chapter-sext]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 265 (IV Sunday in Lent, At Sext)
!Gal 4:27
Rejoice, thou barren that bearest not; break forth and cry, thou that travailest not; * for the desolate hath many more children than she which hath an husband.
R. Thanks be to God.

[benedictus-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 266 (IV Week in Lent, Monday)
Take these things hence, * saith the Lord, and make not my Father's house an house of merchandise.

[magnificat-antiphon-monday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 266 (IV Week in Lent, Monday)
Destroy this temple, * saith the Lord, and in three days I will raise it up: but he spake of the temple of his body.

[benedictus-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, pp. 266–267 (IV Week in Lent, Tuesday)
Why go ye about * to kill me, a man that hath told you the truth?

[magnificat-antiphon-tuesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 267 (IV Week in Lent, Tuesday)
But no man * laid hands on him, because his hour was not yet come.

[benedictus-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 267 (IV Week in Lent, Wednesday)
Master, * wherein hath this man sinned, that he was born blind? Jesus answered and said: Neither hath this man sinned, nor his parents; but that the works of God should be manifest in him.

[magnificat-antiphon-wednesday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 267 (IV Week in Lent, Wednesday)
A man * that is called Jesus made clay out of spittle and anointed mine eyes, and now I see.

[benedictus-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 267 (IV Week in Lent, Thursday)
Jesus went * into a city called Nain: and behold there was a dead man carried out, the only son of his mother.

[magnificat-antiphon-thursday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 268 (IV Week in Lent, Thursday)
A great prophet * is risen up among us, and God hath visited his people.

[benedictus-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 268 (IV Week in Lent, Friday)
Our friend * Lazarus sleepeth: let us go, that we may awake him out of sleep.

[magnificat-antiphon-friday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 268 (IV Week in Lent, Friday)
Lord, if thou hadst been here, * Lazarus would not have died: lo, by this time he stinketh; for he hath lain in the grave four days already.

[benedictus-antiphon-saturday]
# SOURCE: monastic diurnal, p. 268 (IV Week in Lent, Saturday)
He that followeth me * shall not walk in darkness, but shall have the light of life, saith the Lord.
