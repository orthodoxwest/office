# SOURCE: Monastic Diurnal.pdf, p. 635 (October 27, Vigil of Sts Simon and Jude)
Grant, we beseech thee, Almighty God: that like as we approach the glorious birthday of thine Apostles Simon and Jude, so they may approach thy Majesty for us, that we may be made worthy of all thy benefits.
