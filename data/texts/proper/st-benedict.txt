[benedictus-antiphon]
# SOURCE: parish Sanctoral Lauds booklet, PDF p. 81 (St. Benedict; Sanctissime Confessor)
Benedict, * thou father and guide of monks, thou most holy Confessor of the Lord, intercede for us all and for our salvation.

[magnificat-antiphon-first]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 351 (St. Benedict, I Vespers; Exsultet omnium)
Let all the multitude * of the faithful exult in the glory of the gracious Father Benedict: and chiefly let the company of monks be joyful, celebrating his festival on earth, in whose goodly fellowship the Saints rejoice in heaven.

[magnificat-antiphon]
# SOURCE: draft archdiocesan Vespers book, p. 351 (St. Benedict, II Vespers; Hodie sanctus Benedictus)
Today holy Benedict, * while his disciples beheld it, ascended by way of the East on a straight pathway to heaven: today with hands uplifted he died between the words of his supplication: today he was received into glory by the Angels.

[collect]
# SOURCE: parish Sanctoral Lauds booklet, PDF p. 82 (St. Benedict, Collect)
Almighty and everlasting God, who as on this day didst release thy most venerable Confessor Benedict from the prison-house of the flesh, that thou mightiest lift him up to heaven: we beseech thee, grant unto us thy servants who keep this festival the pardon of all our sins; that we, who with glad hearts rejoice in his glory, may at his intercession with thee be made partakers of his everlasting reward.

[hymn-lauds]
# SOURCE: parish Sanctoral Lauds booklet, PDF pp. 80-81 (St. Benedict; Laudibus cives resonent canoris)
Laudibus cives resonent canoris

Gem of the highest, diadem immortal,
Cherished for ever after sacred struggle,
Thou, among many, first in lofty merit,
Benedict, shinest.

Thee in thy boyhood holy eld adorned;
Surely of nothing hath desire despoiled thee;
Earth's blossom whithered, to thy heart uplifted
Unto the highest.

Fleeing in secret, thou didst leave thy native
Country and parents: then, a forest dweller,
Into subjection unto Christ, by torment
Broughtest thy body.

Not long securely couldst thou be hidden,
Good deeds and wonders brought thee to men's knowledge;
Through the world widely spread the happy rumour,
Speedily flying.

Praise to the Father, to the Sole-begotten,
And to thee, alway with the Twain co-equal,
Fostering Spirit; one and only Godhead
Through all the ages.

Amen.
