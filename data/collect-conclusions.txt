# Collect conclusion forms, per XII Of the Collects in the Monastic Diurnal's
# General Rubrics (PDF p. 29, printed p. xxxi). The same rule appears as
# XXXIII.4 in diurnal-rubrics.pdf. See data/texts/shared/formulas.txt for the
# formula texts and the quoted rule.
#
# Format:  <corpus-key> <form>
#
# Forms (suffixes of shared/formulas/collect-conclusion-*):
#   through        addressed to the Father, the Son not mentioned (DEFAULT)
#   through-same   the Son is mentioned before, but not at, the ending
#   who-liveth     the Son is mentioned at the ending of the collect
#   who-livest     the collect is ADDRESSED to the Son (second person)
#   ...-spirit     any of the above, when the Holy Spirit is also mentioned
#                  ("in the unity of the SAME Holy Ghost")
#
# "through" is the default and is NOT listed here; only departures are recorded.
# Every collect not named below takes shared/formulas/collect-conclusion-through.
#
# HOW THESE WERE DETERMINED
#
# The complete Monastic Diurnal scan prints the rule and, for most of its
# collects, the appointed abbreviated conclusion ("Through", "Through the
# same", "Who with thee ... liveth", or "Who with God the Father ... livest").
# Its direct printed examples govern wherever the corpus carries that prayer.
# Parish books and supplements govern prayers added or translated differently
# in current parish use. Classification from wording is reserved for prayers
# without a matching printed conclusion.
#
# One genuine book disagreement: proper/st-anne/collect. The Sanctoral Vespers
# book prints "Through &c."; the Sanctoral Lauds book and the diurnal (p. 575)
# print "Through the same &c." Her collect names the Son before the ending, so
# the General Rubrics make the Vespers book's plain "Through" inconsistent.

# --- Ordinary-tier collects ---
# The last-resort fallbacks behind proper-collect, reached on days with no proper
# of their own. Not referenced directly by any hour definition, so the engine
# concludes them. ordinary/prime/collect, ordinary/compline/collect and the
# suffrage and cross collects ARE referenced directly, keep their conclusions
# inline, and must NOT be listed here.
# Note: resolveProperCollectText redirects terce/sext/none to the lauds collect
# slot, so ordinary/{terce,sext,none}/collect are unreachable in practice (see
# data/review/zero-occurrences.csv). They are classified anyway so that the data
# stays correct if that redirect ever changes.
ordinary/none/collect through-same
ordinary/terce/collect through-spirit
