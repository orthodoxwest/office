# nothing here enters occurrence or composes an office. The ordo page and the text ordo print
# each as a bracketed line under the parish office of its date, with its rank and, where the
# ordo gives one, where its office is found. Monastics and oblates pray it from the Diurnal.
#
# Keys: Name, Rank, Month, Day, Office (where the office is found), Source, Notes.
#
# Rank is the ordo's printed letter. How the observance ranks against the parish day it falls
# on (concurrence with the Sunday, an octave, its own octave) is not settled here: the app
# composes no monastic office.
