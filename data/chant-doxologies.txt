# The seasonal and proper hymn endings (doxologies) cut into sung syllables, so a hymn's score
# can sing whichever ending the day takes to the notes of its own last stanza. The engine puts an
# ending here in place of a hymn's own last stanza (engine.rs, substitute_hymn_doxology); a whole
# hymn's score then sets the ending's syllables, in order, under the notes it prints for its own
# last stanza, where both have the same count (Long Metre: four lines of eight).
#
# Each [key] names a corpus ending; its lines are the ending's words, `|` between the syllables
# of a word (a hyphen stays part of the word: Vir|gin-|born). `make validate` checks the words
# against the corpus text and that every ending the engine can substitute has a row.
