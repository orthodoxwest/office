# Sabina and Felicitas commemoration antiphons and versicles

The 2026 Ordo pp.93,118 explicitly appoints "The kingdom of heaven" at
the preceding Vespers and "Give her" at Lauds for Sabina and Felicitas.
Both are the Holy Women commemoration forms on Diurnal p.5*, with their
own "In thy grace" and "Full of grace" versicles. The generic male Martyr
Common previously supplied all four appointments.

ProperID mappings now select dedicated proper aliases to the existing
Holy Women texts. These declarations implement only the unambiguous
antiphon and versicle appointments; no new prayer wording or engine rule
is introduced. The current collect fallbacks remain unchanged pending
the separately documented source questions:

- Issue412: Sabina's Ordo collect citation points to the Virgin Martyr
  form on pp.48*–49*, whereas its non-Virgin counterpart is on p.53*.
- Issue413: Felicitas's Ordo collect is assigned to a supplement. A
  candidate exists in the draft Vicariate Missal, but its appointment and
  wording for the Office still need an authoritative witness or confirmation.
