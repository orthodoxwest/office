Test the Android app

The Daily Office for Android is nearly ready for the Play Store. Before Google will list it, at least twelve people must test it for fourteen days in a row. Thank you for being one of them.

It takes two links, with a short wait between them. The first puts you on the testers’ list; the Play Store then needs a few minutes to hear of it before the second link will let you in. Keep this page open so you can come back to it.

Open this page on your Android phone, so that every step uses the Google account your Play Store is signed in to. A personal Gmail account works best; work and school accounts are often not allowed to join.

  1. Join the testers’ group

    Open the testers’ group

    Tap Join group. It sends no email. Then come back to this page.

  2. Wait a few minutes

    Google takes a little while to tell the Play Store that you have joined, usually a few minutes and now and then an hour or more. If you open the next link too soon, it will say you can’t join or the app isn’t available. Nothing is wrong: wait a while and try again.

  3. Become a tester

    Open the tester sign-up

    Tap Become a tester, then Download it on Google Play, and install the app. If the Play Store can’t find the app straight away, it is the same wait: try again in a few minutes.

  4. Keep it for two weeks

    Keep the app installed for at least fourteen days, and please don’t tap Leave the program: leaving and joining again starts the fourteen days over. Updates come through the Play Store like any other app’s.

If something goes wrong

If the tester sign-up still turns you away after an hour, check that you joined the group with the same Google account your Play Store uses. Tap your picture at the top right of the group’s page and of the Play Store to see which account each is using; if they differ, join the group again with the Play Store’s account and wait as before.

If the group won’t let you join, you are probably signed in with a work or school account; switch to your personal Google account and try again.

If you installed the preview from GitHub, keep it if you like: it sits beside the Play Store app, but only the Play Store app counts toward the test.

Tell us of a problem or a wish with Report a problem at the foot of any hour, or through the project’s issue tracker.
