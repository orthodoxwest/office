    About the counts

    Approximate browser counts, not people or completed prayers. All reporting days use America/New_York. Zero means no recorded visits.

    What is counted?

    A browser counts once per day in each category it uses. Home contributes to all visitors; office counts overlap. Ordo counts a calendar page view. Reminders counts a generated feed link.

    Only today’s Office counts, after someone interacts with the page or lingers on it. Dated archives, preloads and self-identifying crawlers are excluded. Offline visits and browsers without JavaScript are missed; separate devices or cleared cookies can count again.

    The daily average includes zero days and excludes today. A zero may also mean collection was not yet active; the report cannot distinguish this from a quiet day.

    How are breakdowns measured?

    Each browser using a category on three days contributes three browser-days. Percentages divide each category’s count by the sum for that breakdown. Weekly totals cover Monday–Sunday; weekly percentages use summed counts, not averages of daily percentages. Today and its week are still in progress. Gaps mean no observations.

    Prayer forms describe the form used, not the reader’s ordination. A browser using multiple forms in one day counts in each.

    Appearance and screen describe the page when a visit counted. Nave is light; Apse is dark, whether chosen or inherited from the device. Mobile means a viewport under 700px or a touch-primary pointer, including tablets and phones in landscape.

    Each browser counts once per value per day. Switching appearance or screen category can count on both sides. Percentages use the reported browser-days for that pair; they do not partition all visitors. Older app versions may not report these categories.
