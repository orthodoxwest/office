    Reading these figures

    • A reader is one browser or app installation on one day: not a person, and not a completed prayer. Two devices are two readers.
    • Days are Eastern time. Today is still filling, and is left out of every average.
    • New means first counted on that device; clearing site data or reinstalling makes a reader new again.
    • Hours and settings overlap: a reader who opens Lauds and Vespers counts in both, and one who changes a setting during the day counts under both of its values.
    • A week’s or a month’s readers count each device once in it, without keeping anything that links one day to another.
