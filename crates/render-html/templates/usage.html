    About the counts

    Approximate browser counts, not people or completed prayers. The Android and iOS apps count as browsers do, each installation once a day. All reporting days use America/New_York. Zero means no recorded visits.

    What is counted?

    A browser counts once per day in each category it uses. Home contributes to all visitors; office counts overlap. Ordo counts a calendar page view. Reminders counts a generated feed link.

    Only today’s Office counts, after someone interacts with the page or lingers on it; in the apps, when a page is shown. Dated archives, preloads and self-identifying crawlers are excluded. Offline visits and browsers without JavaScript are missed; separate devices or cleared cookies can count again. In the apps, Reminders counts turning reminders on.

    The report begins with the first day any visit was recorded; a longer period shows only the days since. Last 7 days, and the chart’s line, average the completed days before today, quiet ones included; the line begins once a day has seven recorded days behind it.

    How are breakdowns measured?

    Each browser using a category on three days contributes three browser-days. Percentages divide each category’s count by the sum for that breakdown. Weekly totals cover Monday–Sunday; weekly percentages use summed counts, not averages of daily percentages. Today and its week are still in progress. Gaps mean no observations.

    Prayer forms describe the form used, not the reader’s ordination. A browser using multiple forms in one day counts in each.

    Appearance and screen describe the page when a visit counted. Nave is light; Apse is dark, whether chosen or inherited from the device. Mobile means a viewport under 700px or a touch-primary pointer, including tablets and phones in landscape.

    New or returning: each browser or app installation remembers, on the device, the day it first opened the Office (an app, the day it first reported). It is New on that day and Returning on every later day; only that word is sent, so nothing ties one day to another. Clearing site data or reinstalling counts as new again. Browsers and apps used before this was counted are Returning when they kept a setting, the offline copy or an earlier report; a few without any of those counted as new once. New readers in the summary are the New counts over the last seven completed days.

    Client separates a browser tab, the web app installed to a home screen, and the Android and iOS apps.

    Martyrology at Prime counts Prime readers on days with a Martyrology reading: Shown when their Settings choice read it, Hidden when it left the rubric in its place (the default). Other hours and pages do not report it.

    Each browser counts once per value per day. Switching appearance or screen category can count on both sides. Percentages use the reported browser-days for that breakdown; they do not partition all visitors. Older app versions may not report these categories.
