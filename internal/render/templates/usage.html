Reading these numbers

These are approximate browser counts, not completed prayers. Office, Ordo and Reminders columns overlap; they do not add up to the daily total. Ordo counts a viewed calendar page; Reminders counts a generated feed link, not a visit to the reminders page. Home also contributes to the total but has no column of its own.

Appearance and screen are recorded as the page was rendered at the moment it counted, once per browser per day, each under its own named dimension so a pair added or retired later cannot disturb the others. The band shows each day’s mix and the percentages beside it sum the period as browser-days, so both compare with each other rather than with the daily totals above. Apse covers a dark appearance whether chosen or inherited from the device; Mobile covers a viewport under 700px or any touch-primary pointer, so tablets and a phone in landscape count there too. A reader who switches appearance or rotates a phone on different days counts on both sides.

Only today’s Office counts, and only once someone touches the page or lingers on it. Reading the dated archive, preloading, and self-identifying crawlers are all excluded, so the site stays open to scraping without it showing up here.

Offline visits and browsers without JavaScript are missed; separate devices or cleared cookies can count again. Zero means no recorded visits. All reporting days use America/New_York.
