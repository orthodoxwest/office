Reading these numbers

These are approximate browser counts, not completed prayers. Office columns overlap; they do not add up to the daily total. Home, Ordo and reminders also contribute to the total.

Preloading does not count. Offline visits and browsers without JavaScript are missed; separate devices or cleared cookies can count again. Zero means no recorded visits. All reporting days use America/New_York.
